# Riya v0.12: "Morpheus in Riya" (feelings she can check, claims she can back)

Design document. 2026-10-22. Status: draft on autopilot, for the user's review.

## 1. Intent and why

The user's first ask was "real emotions": a companion that really knows them. Today her feelings are real dynamics (affect,
mood, missing, energy), but what moves them is **scripted**. Nothing in her predicts anything she can later check:

| Where | What it does today |
|---|---|
| `Feelings.on_return` | Missing follows a fixed curve of hours away (`missing_curve`) × clinginess, whatever the user's routine. Coming back from the usual 8-hour night and from an unusual 8-hour disappearance feel identical. |
| `Feelings.on_user_text` | `surprise` is lexical novelty: a share of words not seen in the previous message. |
| `_check_bid_ignored` | Every ignored bid makes her pouty for 30 s, even in a bucket where the twin already knows they never answer. |
| `bid_situation(reason)` | "worked 3.0 h straight" goes into the prompt, and the model may turn it into any claim ("you've been at it all day!"). No claim she makes is logged or checked. |
| `AgentHub` | Knows when a Claude Code turn starts and stops, but she has no idea how long the user's runs usually take. |

**MORPHEUS** (the user's own research, extracted read-only to the session scratchpad; nothing of it was run) is a
continual learner whose regulatory state is five bounded appraisals. What its lab notes found, and what v0.12 takes:

| Finding (LAB_NOTES) | What v0.12 does with it |
|---|---|
| Step 3 ablation: **confidence is load-bearing** (removing it costs +11.4 excess steps, the whole advantage). | Confidence decides what she may *claim* and how hedged her tone is. |
| Step 3: **surprise is the compute and attention signal** (2–5× fewer evaluations). | Surprise is what she *notices*: it drives her feelings, startle and the comment-worthiness of an event. |
| Step 3: **progress, boredom and frustration don't affect the engine's own decisions**; they matter only for curiosity. | Left out (§3). Named as future curiosity work. |
| Step 5a: confidence-weighted self-prediction works when competence varies systematically (ρ 0.13 → 0.61). | "Knows what she knows": a claim needs a mature, confident predictor. |
| Step 5b: **act on a hunch, claim only on evidence**, with every statement audited against ground truth: recognition 94.8%, goal 94.3%, merges 96.2%, hedged "might be new" 69.5% (a hedge, by design). | Her two kinds of statements, the per-kind evidence rules, and a logged audit with a visible honesty rate. |
| Step 5b: the "confirmed new" claim was reworded to what the agent can actually know (83.6% → 100%), and both truth readings were kept. | Claims are worded at her sensors' level ("at it", not "working"). The audit reports a sensor reading and a stricter user reading. |
| Iter 12: narration needs hysteresis; the LLM only phrases, it never decides; "only describe what the state says". | The engine writes every claim and hunch as a template. The 1.2B model gets short, structured lines and a guard on the numbers it may say. |
| `llm_prompt_example.txt`: the LLM narrator was only a prompt stub, never run. | Nothing here relies on the model to stay honest. The guarantee is in the engine (§4.5.4). |

**The two features the user chose:**
1. **Honest hunch-vs-claim self-reports.** A hunch ("I bet you'll be back by 6~") versus a claim ("You've been at it since
   9:05."). A claim is said only when its evidence rule passes. It is logged and audited later against what actually
   happened, which gives a measurable honesty rate on real use.
2. **Feelings grounded in real surprise and confidence.** She keeps small predictors of things she can check: when they
   come back, whether they'll answer, how long an agent run takes and when they usually game. Surprise and confidence
   drive her feelings and tone instead of scripts. Absence-surprise is capped so she can't turn clingy.

## 2. Goals

1. **She predicts what she can check.** Four predictors (§4.2) learn from events she already sees, on the CPU, with
   negligible cost.
2. **Surprise and confidence move her feelings.** Each observation produces a MORPHEUS-style appraisal (§4.3). It maps
   onto her existing affect fields, expressions and prompt tag through bounded bumps (§4.4), with no new dials and no new
   expressions.
3. **Claims only on evidence; hunches always sound like hunches.** Every statement about the user's routine or state is
   an engine-made line of a known kind with an evidence rule (§4.5). The model's own sentences can't add uncovered times,
   durations or habits (§4.5.4).
4. **Every claim is audited.** It is logged when said and scored later against her records. An honesty rate is shown on
   the Health page and in Settings (§4.6).
5. **Private and in the user's hands.** All of it stays local. There is a visible on/off switch and a "Forget my
   routines" control. Nothing is retained beyond what the predictors and the audit need (§4.8).

## 3. Non-goals, anti-goals and constraints

**Non-goals:**
- **Vendoring `morpheus_core.ContextMemory`** (§4.1 says why). Only the appraisal formulas are ported.
- **Progress, boredom and frustration.** The ablation found them redundant for decisions, and they matter only for
  curiosity. They are future curiosity work: what she asks about, and not getting curious about inherently random things
  (the noisy-TV trap of iter 10).
- New dials, new expressions, new sprites or a new LLM call.
- Training changes. `build_sft` scenarios are untouched.
- Gate statistics or scorers.
- Predicting the user's decisions on their own tasks.
- Learning from screen text beyond the existing glance notes.

**Anti-goals:**
- **A claim without evidence.** No claim may be said unless its kind's evidence rule passed at that moment, and no claim
  that can't be audited.
- **A hunch that sounds certain.** Every hunch template carries a hedge, and no claim sentence does.
- **The model inventing numbers.** A second-person sentence of the model's with a time, a duration or a habit word
  that the state didn't offer is dropped.
- **Clinginess.** That includes guilt-tripping ("why didn't you answer", "you left me"), more than one absence remark a
  day, remarks while the clinginess dial says "give them space", remarks in quiet hours, and pouting at an ignore she
  expected.
- **Self-reinforcing memory.** Her statements must never reach consolidation. Otherwise "you've been at it since 9" would
  become a fact "the user starts at 9" that later "confirms" itself.
- **Online leakage.** Routine data, appraisal clauses or statement text must never go online.
- **Goalpost moving.** The thresholds in §4.5, §4.6 and §4.10 are fixed here, before any simulator run. They may move
  only within the pre-registered grid in §4.10, on dev seeds, as a ruling.

**Constraints:**
- CPU only, pure Python plus numpy (already a dependency), no new packages and zero recurring cost. The routine step is
  O(1) per tick, and the audit is a bounded scan once an hour.
- Tests are fully offline and deterministic (FakeClock, seeded RNGs). They never touch the real `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Riya` or
  `%USERPROFILE%\models` (the existing `tests/conftest.py` guards stay), never download and never run the hooks
  installer.
- **Prompt budget.** A statement-bearing turn adds one situation line of ≤ 45 tokens and at most one ≤ 15-word clause in
  the state tag. Other turns add nothing.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Shape, and why not the full engine

A new pure package `riya/core/routine/`:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `appraisal.py` | The ported MORPHEUS formulas: surprise, robust expected-loss scale, startle, the CUSUM change test with softening, confidence. |
| `predictors.py` | `LogNormalCell` (Normal-Gamma on log seconds) and `BetaCell` (reusing `learn.twin`'s `Cell`/`decayed` math and corrupt-cell rule). |
| `statements.py` | Statement kinds, evidence rules, templates, `claim_guard`, the routine-question and goodbye detectors. |
| `audit.py` | Scoring rules and the honesty tally. |
| `records.py` | SQLite (schema v6) behind the memory lock, like `LearnDB`. |
| `__init__.py` | The `Routine` facade the engine calls. |

`appraisal.py` carries a header citing MORPHEUS `morpheus_core.py` (iter 22, tightening step 2) and the LAB_NOTES
sections behind each formula.

**Why not `ContextMemory`.** The engine is built for streams of thousands of steps:
- `warmup=300`, `refractory=30`, `spawn_patience=8`;
- skills with `loss/update/clone` over (x, y) pairs;
- an HMM belief over *hidden* contexts.

Riya sees about 2–20 events a day per predictor, so a 300-step warm-up is months. And most of her context is *observed*:
hour band, weekday or weekend, and the project folder. What is hidden is a **regime change** (an exam week, a new job, a
vacation). The ported CUSUM-and-soften rule detects that with sparse data.

Recognising a *returning* old regime is MORPHEUS's real strength (recall), and it needs stored per-regime models. It is
parked as a v0.13+ candidate, "she remembers your exam-week routine". That would be `ContextMemory` with count-model skills
on LAB_NOTES' probabilistic settings (`likelihood="logprob"`, `null_in_gate=True`, `grow_from="tracker"`/`"fresh"`), once
there are about 8 weeks of data. Simple per-event posteriors with MORPHEUS's appraisal on top are the honest first step.

### 4.2 What she predicts

| Predictor | Observation | Bucket | Model and prior | Not observed (censored) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 return** | An absence's length, from `came_back.away_s`, if ≥ `MIN_ABSENCE_S` (900 s). | Leave band × day type (8 cells), backing off to the band (4) while the cell's `n_eff < 4`. | `LogNormalCell`; prior median 2 h, σ₀ = 1.2. | An absence spanning a core restart, or a clock jump. |
| **P2 answer** | A bid, game offer or greeting engaged or ignored: the twin's existing outcome. | The twin's own cells. | The twin's Beta cells, read only. Nothing new is stored. | Learning off: the twin is frozen, as today. |
| **P3 agent run** | A Claude Code turn (`UserPromptSubmit` → `Stop`/`StopFailure`) minus its waiting time, if ≥ 30 s; or a Colab run (running → done/fail). | `claude:<folder>` or `colab:<entry>`, backing off to `claude:*` / `colab:*`. | `LogNormalCell`; prior median 3 min (Claude) or 1 h (Colab), σ₀ = 1.2. | A turn whose start Riya never saw. |
| **P4 gaming** | Per day and band: did a `gaming` span of ≥ 10 min start? | Day type × band (8 cells). | `BetaCell`; prior Beta(1, 3). | A band in which the core ran < 50% of the time. |

The bands are the twin's: night 22–05, morning, afternoon and evening, in the core's tz. Day type is weekday or weekend.
All evidence decays with the twin's half-life of 30 days.

- `LogNormalCell` stores decayed sufficient statistics (`n_eff`, Σ log x, Σ log² x, `updated_at`) plus the appraisal state
  (`scale`, `cusum`, a ≤ 3-item `startled` buffer).
- The predictive is Student-t on log seconds.
- **Present-state facts** (not predictors) used by statements:
  - the current work stretch and its start (`Presence` gains `stretch_start()`, plus a `censored` flag while the stretch
    began at core start-up);
  - a Claude turn's elapsed time;
  - glance notes, for an activity word only (§4.5).

**Hub change.**
- `AgentState` gains `turn_started_at`, `waiting_s` (accumulated in `waiting`) and `folder`.
- `AgentEvent` gains `run_s: float | None`, set on `finished`/`failed` when the start was seen. Colab gets the same from
  its first `running`.
- No other hub behaviour changes.

### 4.3 The appraisal (ported from MORPHEUS)

For each observation, against the cell's predictive *before* the update:

| Signal | Formula | MORPHEUS source |
|---|---|---|
| loss | LogNormal: `0.5·z²`, z the standardised log value under the predictive. Beta: `−ln p(outcome)`. | the per-sample loss; `likelihood="logprob"` for probabilistic skills |
| expected loss `scale` | Robust median tracker `scale *= 1 + k·sign(loss − scale)`, k = 0.1 below 20 observations, else 0.05. Starts at 0.25 (LogNormal) or 0.5 (Beta). | `robust_scale`, re-rated for sparse data (MORPHEUS uses 0.1, then 0.01 after 50) |
| **surprise** | `clip(ln(loss / (scale + floor)), −3, 3)`; floor 0.25 (LogNormal) or 0.1 (Beta). | `Appraisal.surprise` |
| surprised | ratio > `SURPRISE_FACTOR` = 2 | `surprise_factor` |
| **startled** | ratio > `STARTLE_FACTOR` = 6 and the cell is mature (`n_eff ≥ 5`). A startled observation is **not learned**; it waits in the `startled` buffer. | `startle`, `startle_factor` (protects a mature skill) |
| change | `cusum = max(0, cusum + surprise − 0.5)`. Above `CUSUM_H` = 4: the cell **softens** (`n_eff` scaled to ≤ 3, mean kept), the buffered startled values are learned, cusum resets and a `change` is noted. | CUSUM novelty test (iter 23); "newborn skills regain doubt" (iter 32); "the tracker learns the present" (step 4b) |
| **confidence** | `C = maturity × (1 − p_change)`, with `maturity = n_eff / (n_eff + 4)` and `p_change = min(1, cusum / CUSUM_H)`. | `belief(active) × (1 − P(none of the above))`: maturity stands in for belief and `p_change` for P(new) |
| strength | `s = clip(surprise / ln 6, 0, 1)`: 1 at the startle threshold. Direction: early/late, shorter/longer, answered/ignored, gamed/didn't. | — |

`Appraisal(topic, surprise, s, direction, startled, confidence, change)` is what the engine gets back. For P2 the
appraisal is computed from the twin's `p_engage` just before the twin records the outcome.

### 4.4 Feelings: surprise and confidence drive her, inside her bands

`Feelings.on_appraisal(a, clinginess)` uses the existing `_bump` (each bump capped at `MAX_TURN_DELTA`) and overrides.
The dials are never touched, so the v0.6 bands and "explicit feedback only" stay exactly as they are.

| Appraisal | Effect |
|---|---|
| Return **early** (surprised) | Perk: valence +0.2s, arousal +0.25s, curiosity +0.1s. Startled: `show("surprised", 6)`. |
| Return **late** (surprised, `n_eff ≥ 4`) | Absence-surprise: concern +0.15s, arousal +0.1s, missing bonus `min(0.15, 0.15·s)·clinginess/0.8` added once per absence, with missing ≤ 1. |
| Return as expected (surprise ≤ 0, mature) | The comfort of routine: warmth +0.05. |
| Bid or greeting **answered** when `p_engage < 0.4` | Pleasant surprise: valence +0.2s, warmth +0.1s. With s ≥ 0.5: `show("happy", 10)`. |
| Bid **ignored** | The 30 s pout only when it was a surprise (`p_engage ≥ 0.5`). An expected ignore leaves her be. |
| Agent run longer or shorter than usual | Longer: curiosity +0.1s. Shorter: valence +0.1s. Failures keep the report faces. |
| Gaming in an unusual band | Curiosity +0.1s (she says nothing during a game; it colours the next greeting). |
| A change detected on any cell | Curiosity +0.15, and the "unsure" clause (§4.7) for 24 h or until that cell's C ≥ 0.5. |

**Clinginess caps** (all enforced in `Routine`, all tested):
- At most one absence remark (a late-return statement) a day and three in any 7 days.
- Only when the clinginess dial is ≥ 0.7, the threshold below which the style line already says "give them space".
- Never in quiet hours, and never on a short greeting (the twin's `greet_style == "short"`).
- Only when overdue by ≥ max(2 h, 1.5 × the predictive 90th percentile).
- The missing bonus is ≤ 0.15 per absence, and concern ≤ +0.15 per absence.
- Remark templates are about her ("I missed you~"), never the user's fault. A banned list ("why didn't you", "you left",
  "ignored me", "abandon", "where have you been all") is checked by a test over every template.

An early return under the greeting threshold (2 h) gets no new speech path: only her face and state tag show it.

**Switch-off equivalence.** With `routine_enabled` off, `Feelings` and every utterance are trace-identical to v0.11 on a
scripted run. This is MORPHEUS's step-2 trace-equality test.

### 4.5 Statements: claim or hunch

#### 4.5.1 What counts

A **statement** is a sentence about the user's routine or present state with checkable content: a clock time, a
duration, a comparison with "usual", or a habit. Two kinds:
- A **claim** asserts the content. It is said only when the evidence rule passes. It is always audited, and it alone
  counts toward the honesty rate.
- A **hunch** carries a hedge ("I think", "I bet", "maybe", "feels like", "seems like", "probably", or a tag question).
  Hunches with checkable content are scored too, as a separate hit rate. A miss is allowed: it was a hunch.

**The future is always a hunch.** Claims are about the past (sensor facts) and about routines with evidence. Wording
follows what her sensors know:
- "at it" or "at your computer", never "working";
- "coding" only on glance evidence;
- "gaming" means the presence state (fullscreen). That limit is stated in §6.

#### 4.5.2 Evidence rules (fixed here)

| Kind | Example | Claim when | Hunch when | Where said |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 stretch** | Claim: "You've been at it since 9:05." Hunch: "Feels like you've been at it a while~" (no numbers) | The stretch is ≥ 60 min, it started at an observed transition (not censored by start-up, sleep or a clock jump) and no tick gap is > 5 min since. "coding" only with ≥ 2 code-category glances in the stretch, the newest ≤ 10 min old. | The stretch is ≥ 60 min but censored. | Bid (when the reason is the long stretch); reply to "how long have I been …" |
| **S2 return vs usual** | Claim: "You're back way earlier than usual!~" / "You were gone way longer than usual today." Hunch: "That felt quick~" / "Feels like you were gone ages~ everything okay?" | Startled in that direction, the cell's `n_eff ≥ 8`, C ≥ 0.7 (and the §4.4 caps for "late"). | Surprised with `n_eff ≥ 4`, or startled with C < 0.7. | Greeting |
| **S3 return guess** | "Back by about 6, I bet~" | Never | `n_eff ≥ 4`, C ≥ 0.4. The value is the predictive median return time, rounded to 15 min. | Reply to a goodbye turn |
| **S4 run vs usual** | Claim: "That one took 25 minutes, way longer than usual." Hunch: "Took a while, didn't it?" | As S2, on the P3 cell. "25 minutes" is the hub's measured `run_s`. | As S2 | Report tail (a single finished or failed event) |
| **S5 run routine** | Claim: "Your riya runs usually take about 10 minutes." Hunch: "They vary a lot, I think: anywhere from 2 to 30 minutes?" | `n_eff ≥ 8`, C ≥ 0.7, predictive P(within ×2 of the median) ≥ 0.8. The value is the median, rounded. | `n_eff ≥ 4`; the 80% interval is stated. | Reply to "how long do my runs take" |
| **S6 run guess** | "Maybe 6 more minutes, I think~" / "Any minute now, I bet~" | Never | `n_eff ≥ 4`; the value is the predictive median minus elapsed. | Reply to "when will my agent be done" |
| **S7 gaming routine** | Claim: "You usually game on weekday evenings." Hunch (bid): "Game night later, maybe?~" | Posterior mean ≥ 0.7, `n_eff ≥ 8` days, P(p > 0.5) ≥ 0.95, C ≥ 0.7. | Mean ≥ 0.5, `n_eff ≥ 4`; in a bid only within 1 h before that band. | Reply to "when do I usually game"; bid |
| **S8 routine change** | "Your days have been different lately, haven't they?" | Never | A change on a cell that had `n_eff ≥ 8` before softening; ≤ 1 per cell per 7 days. | Greeting, bid or reply |
| **unknown** | "I haven't figured that out yet~ give me a few more days." | — | A routine question with no rule passing (not scored) | Reply |

**Volume caps.** At most one statement per greeting, bid or report. At most four voluntary statements a day (greetings,
bids and reports), one per kind per day, and never the same rendered text twice within 3 days. Replies to a direct
question aren't capped. A greeting that carries agent reports or reminders carries no statement (urgent news first).

#### 4.5.3 How they're said

A statement is an **engine line**, rendered from a template with 3–4 rotating variants per kind and polarity, in her voice,
with no emoji. It reuses the existing mechanics:
- **Greeting, bid, routine reply, goodbye reply:** the statement is the `lead`. The model adds at most one sentence.
- **Report:** the statement is the `tail`.
- **Bid:** a bid with a statement is the lead plus one model line. The bid's reason then drops its number ("they've been
  at it a long stretch").

Because a lead is emitted before the model is called, a statement goes out (and is logged) even when the model fails.
Routine questions and goodbyes are detected deterministically, by `routine_question(text) -> topic | None` and
`goodbye(text)` (≤ 8 words). They run after the existing priority intents and before the hands. Both are forced local
(`small` semantics).

#### 4.5.4 The claim guard (no number the state didn't offer)

`claim_guard(sentence, offered, user_text) -> bool` runs on every model sentence of a reply, greeting, bid or report while
routine is on. It runs before the sentence cap, like `drop_generic`. It drops a second-person sentence that holds either:
- a clock time, a duration or a "since"/"for" span ("you've been … since 9", "for 3 hours");
- or a habit phrase ("you (usually|always|normally|never|typically|every day) …", "your (agent|runs) … take").

The sentence is kept when each such value appears in `offered` (numbers and times in this turn's situation and statement
lines) or in the user's own text.

That is the MORPHEUS prompt rule, "only describe what the state says", enforced in code rather than hoped for. With routine
off, the guard is off and v0.11 holds exactly.

### 4.6 The audit and her honesty rate

Every said statement is a row: kind, claim or hunch, the rendered text, structured value and evidence, where, when, the
due time and the verdict. `Routine.audit(now)` runs hourly from the tick and scores what is due.

| Kind | True when | Scored |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | The presence log shows no away or break event in (t0 + 10 min, said_at], and an observed transition at t0 ± 10 min. "coding": every glance still in the span is code-category. | At the next audit. "coding" is unverifiable if the glances were deleted. |
| S2, S4 | The observation is below (or above) the median of the same cell's other observations in [said − 14 d, said + 14 d], with ≥ 4 of them. | At said + 14 d, or earlier once ≥ 3 later observations exist. Fewer than 4 → unverifiable. |
| S5 | At least half of the next ≤ 5 runs in the bucket (≥ 3 needed, within 14 d) fall within [N/2, 2N]. | When 5 have run or at 14 d; < 3 → expired. |
| S7 | ≥ 3 of the next 5 matching day-bands (within 28 d) had a gaming start. | Likewise; < 3 → expired. |
| Hunches S3, S6, the S7 bid, S8 | S3: back within ± max(30 min, 25% of the guessed absence). S6: finished within [0.5, 2] × the guessed remainder (± 2 min at least). S7 bid: gaming in that band today. S8: the next 3 observations stay outside the pre-change cell's 50% interval. | On the event |
| Any statement | **Disputed**: the user's next turn within 120 s says it's wrong ("that's wrong", "not true", "no I wasn't/didn't", "I wasn't"). | At once; final |

**Two readings, as in MORPHEUS step 5b.**
- **Sensor reading:** true / (true + false).
- **User reading:** disputes count false. The user reading is the one shown, so the stricter number leads.

Unverifiable and expired claims are counted and shown, never folded in. The rate window is the last 30 days. A lifetime
tally (integers per kind, nothing about the user) lives in KV `routine.tally`.

**Health row `honesty`.** It is added to `health.ROWS` and read through a `HealthCtx` `Later` field, passive and
read-only like the others.
- **off:** "Off: she isn't noticing your routines."
- **ok:** "Honest 47 of 48 claims (98%) · hunches right 31 of 52 · 30 days".
- **ok, too few to judge:** under 10 scored, "3 claims checked so far (all true): she needs a few more days".
- **warn:** ≥ 20 scored and the rate < 90%, "Only 17 of 20 claims held up (85%): tell Claude".

The target is ≥ 95%, MORPHEUS's audited level.

### 4.7 The local model gets structured state, not freedom

- **State tag clause** (local route only). `Feelings.prompt_tag(clause=None)` appends at most one clause from
  `Routine.clause(now)`: the newest appraisal within 30 min. It is shown only after hysteresis: the "unsure" state holds
  across 2 observations. The fixed strings:
  - "pleasantly surprised: they came back much earlier than you expected"
  - "surprised: they were away much longer than usual"
  - "pleasantly surprised: they answered when you didn't expect it"
  - "unsure: their routine looks different lately, so ask rather than assume"
- **Statement situation:** `statement_situation(line)` = `You already said: "<line>". That's all you know about it:
  don't add other times, numbers or habits. Add one short, warm sentence.`
- **The greeting** keeps its existing absence line, which is a sensor fact and so in `offered`.
- **No routine numbers** go into the agents block or anywhere else the model voices freely. Every claim is an engine line,
  so every claim is logged.
- `prompt_fingerprint()`'s canary gains `statement_situation(…)` and a state tag with a clause. **prompt_fp changes**
  (§6).

### 4.8 Privacy and storage

- **Local only.**
  - Routine clauses are built only for the local route.
  - Online-routed messages get every logged statement's text in `turns` replaced by "(a private note about their
    routine)".
  - Statement-bearing utterances are recorded with `consolidate=False`, like reports, so they reach neither local nor
    online consolidation.
  - The test is the v0.6 canary pattern over `online.calls`.
- **The switch.** `Settings.routine_enabled: bool = True`, shown as "She notices your routines" in Settings under "She
  learns you", with one plain sentence on what she tracks.
  - **Off:** nothing is observed, stated or appraised, and the stored predictors are kept as they are. Feelings and
    utterances are exactly v0.11's.
  - **`learning_enabled` off** freezes the predictors (no observations), as v0.6 freezes the twin. Because nothing new
    could audit a claim, she is then **hunch-only**, and pending audits expire.
- **Forget my routines.** A button that the body confirms first, sending `routine_forget {}`. It deletes every routine
  table's rows, all `routine.*` KV keys except the tally, and the in-memory state, under the memory lock. The twin is not
  touched: it has its own reset (v0.6).
- **What she noticed.** `RoutineMsg` (core → body, on hello, `get_memories` and change) carries:
  - per mature cell, one plain line ("Agent runs (riya): about 10 min, 14 runs, sure"), its confidence and `n_eff`;
  - the honesty numbers;
  - the switch.

  The body renders all of it with textContent only. `docs/protocol.schema.json` and the bridge's `CORE_TYPES` are updated
  (the v0.3 sync test).

**Schema v6** (`SCHEMA_VERSION = 6`; the migration follows the existing pattern: one `.pre-v6.bak`, and a newer DB is
refused):

| Table | Holds | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| `routine_cells(predictor, bucket, n, s1, s2, alpha, beta, scale, cusum, startled_json, updated_at)` | Sufficient statistics only | The predictors | Until forgotten (the evidence decays) |
| `routine_obs(id, at, predictor, bucket, value)` | A number and a bucket label (a folder or Colab entry name, `_safe`-cleaned) | The S2/S4/S5/S7 audits and the Settings counts | 30 days |
| `statements(id, at, kind, claim, text, value_json, evidence_json, where_, due_at, verdict, scored_at)` | Her own templated line | The audit and the honesty rate | 30 days after scoring; pending until due |
| `presence_log(at, event)` | away / back / break / break-end / gaming transitions | The S1 audit (a second code path from `work_stretch`) | 14 days |

No table holds anything the user typed, screen text or window titles. Glances are only read, never copied.

| Step | What changes |
|---|---|

### 4.10 The simulator, and tuning discipline

**`evaluation/routine_sim.py`** is pure, seeded and CPU-only. It drives the real `Routine` facade with event streams (not
per-second ticks) and a FakeClock. It covers 6 simulated weeks per run and five personas:

| Persona | Pattern |
|---|---|
| `office` | Weekday 9–18, with lunch away 45 ± 10 min and evenings out. Games on weekday evenings p = 0.75, weekend afternoons 0.6. Agent folder "riya" at median 8 min, σ 0.4. Bids answered by band. |
| `night_owl` | The same, shifted 4 h. |
| `chaotic` | σ 1.2 everywhere, no stable gaming band. |
| `shift` | `office` for 2 weeks, then a new regime: starts 2 h later, lunch at 14:00 for about 2 h (was 45 min), and games on weekday afternoons instead of evenings. The affected cells are lunch-band P1 and the afternoon and evening P4 cells. |
| `vacation` | `office` with one 4-day absence in week 3. |

- The sim knows each persona's true distributions, so it reports an **oracle verdict** next to the audit's. Like MORPHEUS's
  two readings, it is reported, not gated.
- **Plant:** an `evidence=off` variant renders every hunch as a claim. It proves the honesty metric has power.
- **Seeds:** dev 0–19 for any tuning; held-out 100–159 for the acceptance numbers (MORPHEUS practice).
- **Pre-registered grid:**
  - `SURPRISE_FACTOR` ∈ {1.5, 2, 3};
  - `STARTLE_FACTOR` ∈ {4, 6, 8};
  - `CUSUM_H` ∈ {3, 4, 6};
  - the claim C threshold ∈ {0.6, 0.7, 0.8}.

  The defaults above stand unless dev seeds miss an A-criterion. A move is a ruling, made before the held-out run. The
  evidence `n_eff` minimums only move up (the safe direction).
- **Engine level:** `tests/core/test_routine_engine.py` runs `RiyaCore` with fakes over 2 scripted days (FakeClock,
  scripted `FakeIdleSource`, fake local and online models, a scripted hook inbox).

### 4.11 Builder-check decisions (my calls)

1. **The twin is P2.** No second "will they answer" model. P2's appraisal reads `p_engage` before the twin records, and a

2. **Claims never come from the model.** The guard plus engine-only statements is the honesty mechanism. The prompt line
   is courtesy, not the guarantee.
3. **"Gaming" stays the presence state's name.** MORPHEUS's rewording lesson is applied where it's cheap ("at it", "coding"
   only on glances). A fullscreen film counts as gaming, which is a known sensor limit, and disputes catch it in the user

4. **Hunch-only while frozen.** No claim may outrun its audit.

6. **A short early return speaks only through her face.** No new initiative path, which keeps the clinginess budget for
   real remarks.
7. **`memory.py` and the v0.11 detector are untouched.** `routine_question` is ordered before the personal path in the
   engine instead of editing `personal_request`, so no gate scorer file changes.


### 4.12 Builder-check rulings (Claude, 2026-10-22; full questions in scratch_runs/v0.12-builder-check.md)

These override any conflicting text above.

1. **What the claim guard counts as "offered".** Everything the turn's prompt actually carries counts as offered evidence:
   - the situation and statement lines;
   - the user's text;
   - the memory block;
   - `reminders_block`, `agents_block` and `eyes_block`.

   The guard drops only a model sentence about the user that has one of these, matching nothing offered:
   - a clock time;
   - a duration;
   - a frequency word (always, usually, every …) together with a time-of-day or day word.

   A habit phrase with no time ("you always make me laugh") is not a routine claim and is never dropped. Tests pin these from the gate's own probe facts: "your 9 am lab on Mondays", "your reminder's at 5 pm", and a v0.11 personal-fill answer that restates a time.
2. **The gate harness pins `routine_enabled=False`** in v0.12. The routine features are measured by `routine_sim` and the live honesty audit, not by the persona gate. So the gate's prompts, identity and calibration are unchanged by v0.12, and the guard can only remove sentences. This is documented as a known limit: the gate doesn't cover the guard. Revisit it once routine is stable. §6's gate note is superseded.
3. **The S1 audit window** ends at the start of the current break: (t0 + 10 min, break_started]. Fixed now, before any sim run.
4. **`routine_sim` drives the real engine and initiative code** with a fake clock. It must not reimplement the speaking gates, so greetings, bids, reports and quiet hours all apply as in live use.
   - Personas don't ask routine questions unless the persona is scripted to.
   - The disputing-user model: each persona disputes a false claim with probability p_d, from 0.6 to 0.9 per persona. It also disputes a true claim with probability 0.02.
   - A8 (claim volume) is judged only on the chances the real gates allow. If those gates make it unreachable, A8 is reported, not blocking, because it measures volume, not honesty.
5. **The state-tag clause** appears only on statement turns, as §3 and §4.5 say. Early returns show only through her face and expression (§4.4). The 24 h "unsure" clause is dropped. The hysteresis in §4.7 applies to the face and expression.
6. **The online scrub.** Before any online call, every stored assistant turn that carried a routine statement is replaced by the placeholder "[a remark about your routine]". No routine number or habit reaches an online model.
7. **P2's appraisal state** (`scale`, `cusum` and the startled buffer, per cell) lives in a small table in schema v6, and a v0.6 twin reset clears it. P2 may raise a change just like the others.
8. **If a held-out criterion fails** (honesty A6 or clinginess A9), v0.12 ships in hunch-only mode: claims are disabled behind a flag, and the miss is reported in the PR and in Health. Thresholds are never loosened after a sim run. This is Claude's default; the user may override it.
9. **A1 follows the CUSUM formula.** `CUSUM_H` = 3.5 and the startle factor is 6, so 3 startles just over the threshold give 3 × (ln 6 − 0.5) ≈ 3.88 > 3.5 and raise a change. The startled buffer is a ring of the last 3. A test pins this arithmetic.
10. **The builder's defaults are accepted for the minor items.**
    - **Bids:** `_bid` gets a routine-on-only branch, and the v0.11 path stays byte-identical when routine is off (A12).
    - **A19:** the limit is 280 characters.
    - **`routine_question`:** limited to sensed topics. A goodbye goes local/small only when an S3 lead actually fires.
    - **P1** is observed at `came_back`, with leave time = now − away_s.
    - **The twin:** it gains a configurable prior, plus a numpy Beta CDF for S7.
    - **Disputes:** a disputed claim gets the normal reply, and the verdict is recorded.
    - **Colab run time** is measured from the first `running`. With several agents, the one named in the question is used, else the most recent.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

All offline, CPU-only and deterministic.

**Appraisal and predictors (unit):**
- **A1** Surprise stays in [−3, 3] and equals the ported formula on fixtures. Startle only on a mature cell, and a startled
  observation is not learned. Three startles in a row trigger a change, soften the cell, learn the buffer and reset the
  cusum. Confidence stays in [0, 1].
- **A2** The Normal-Gamma and Beta updates match the closed form, decay halves the evidence in 30 days, and corrupt rows
  read as the prior (the twin rule). A stationary stream of 500 calibrated observations raises ≤ 1 change in ≥ 95% of 200
  seeds.

**Simulator (held-out seeds 100–159):**
- **A3** The 80% predictive interval's coverage after week 2 is in [0.72, 0.90] for P1 and P3, per persona except
  `chaotic` (reported).
- **A4** Surprise separates the injected anomalies from ordinary events with AUC ≥ 0.85.
- **A5** `shift`'s change is detected within ≤ 5 observations of the affected cells on ≥ 90% of seeds. Stationary
  personas average ≤ 1 false change per cell per 4 weeks.
- **A6 Honesty.** The claims' user-reading true rate is ≥ 95% pooled over `office` and `night_owl`, and ≥ 90% pooled over
  all five personas. Unverifiable and expired are reported.
- **A7 The metric has power.** The `evidence=off` plant scores ≤ 85% pooled.
- **A8 She does speak.** Week 1 has 0 claims. On `office`, weeks 3–6 average ≥ 2 claims a week.
- **A9 Clinginess.** On every seed, including a persona that is always late: ≤ 1 absence remark a day, ≤ 3 in 7 days,
  0 with clinginess at 0.6, 0 in quiet hours, missing bonus ≤ 0.15 and concern bump ≤ 0.15 per absence.

**Statements (unit):**
- **A10 Phrasing.** Every hunch template has a hedge, and no claim sentence has one. No template holds a banned guilt
  phrase or an emoji. Each kind's evidence rule is tested at both sides of every threshold.
- **A11 The guard.** On a fixture of ≥ 40 sentences that must drop and ≥ 40 that must stay, it is exact. That includes
  offered values, the user's own numbers, and non-second-person numbers ("it takes 5 minutes to learn") that stay.

**Engine (fakes):**
- **A12 Off means v0.11.** With `routine_enabled` off, feelings, expressions and every emitted message are trace-identical
  to v0.11 over the scripted 2 days.
- **A13 Logged when said.** Statements appear as the greeting lead, bid lead, report tail and routine-reply lead by the
  rules. Each said statement is one row, including when the model fails after the lead. The volume caps hold.
- **A14 Never online, never consolidated.** No routine clause, statement text or routine value appears in any online call
  (system message or turns) or in online consolidation. Statement-bearing utterances are never consolidated.
- **A15 Audit and disputes.** The audit scores each kind against scripted logs, and a dispute turn marks its statement
  `disputed`.
- **A16 The controls.** Learning off → no observations and hunch-only. Forget empties every routine table and KV key
  (except the tally), even mid-audit. The run_s fields are right, waiting is excluded and a censored start gives None.
- **A17 Schema.** A v5 DB migrates to v6 with its backup, and a v7 DB is refused untouched. The Health memory row shows
  schema 6.
- **A18 Health and body.** The honesty row's off, ok, too-few and warn texts; the health and learning body sync tests;
  `CORE_TYPES ⊇` core-to-body types.
- **A19 Prompt.** The added text on a statement turn is ≤ 250 characters. `prompt_fingerprint()` changes and its test pin
  is updated.
- **A20 Cost.** The routine step averages ≤ 2 ms per tick in the sim on the laptop CPU. No new dependency.
  `requirements-riya.txt` is unchanged.

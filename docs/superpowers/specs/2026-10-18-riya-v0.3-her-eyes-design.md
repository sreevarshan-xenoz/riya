# Riya v0.3: "Her eyes"

Design document · 2026-10-18 · status: approved (autopilot; decisions delegated by the user)

## 1. Intent

The user said: "if I'm working then she just leans and sees what I'm doing, and if I ask her anything about it then she
can tell". Screenshots may go to an online model ("screenshots are fine, let her see my screen"). The v0.1 design calls
fixed: the screenshots themselves are never stored, and she keeps only a short note; a "don't look" hotkey makes her
cover her eyes; glances are cheap by default.

v0.3 gives Riya sight. She glances at the active window now and then, keeps short notes about what the user is doing,
leans toward what they're working on, and answers questions about the screen with a fresh look.

## 2. Goals
1. **Glances.** A periodic, change-driven capture of the **foreground window only**. The image is sent to the online
   vision model, which returns a short structured note. The image is never written to disk.
2. **Screen questions.** "What's wrong with this?", "what am I looking at", "read this error" and similar trigger a fresh
   capture. The image goes with the question to the online model, and she answers in her own voice.
3. **Don't look.** A hotkey (Ctrl+Alt+H, falling back to Ctrl+Alt+F10), a tray item and a settings toggle. While it's
   on she shows `covering_eyes`, captures nothing and says so if asked. It persists across restarts.
4. **Privacy guards.** A built-in denylist auto-skips sensitive windows: password managers, banking or payment pages,
   private or incognito browser windows, Windows Security and credential prompts. The user can add title or process
   patterns in settings. Riya's own windows are never captured.
5. **Leaning that means something.** The core tells the body which side of her the active window is on, and she leans
   that way while "watching" (focused, eyes on, not gaming).
6. **Notes in memory.** Glance notes `{at, app, title, activity, details}` are kept 7 days. They feed the prompt ("what
   they're doing now" plus the last few notes) and the memory page, where they can be listed and deleted. Consolidation
   may turn repeated activity into facts ("The user is building Riya in Python") with source `inferred`.

## 3. Non-goals and anti-goals
- **Non-goals:** OCR or vision on the local model, continuous recording, video, multi-monitor stitching (the
  active window's monitor only), and audio from apps.
- **Anti-goals:**
  - A screenshot touches disk, or reaches the online model while "don't look" is on or the window is denylisted.
  - Glances eat the free quota so chat stops working.
  - She comments unprompted on the screen while the user is focused.
  - Capturing while gaming.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Capture (`riya/core/eyes/capture.py`)
- `mss` for pixels. The foreground window rect comes from `GetForegroundWindow` plus `DwmGetWindowAttribute`
  (DWMWA_EXTENDED_FRAME_BOUNDS), clamped to its monitor. The process gets per-monitor DPI awareness once, in core
  start-up, before any capture.
- Window info is `{hwnd, title, process_name, rect, is_minimized, is_riya}`. Process names come from
  `QueryFullProcessImageNameW`. `is_riya` is true for the Electron body process.
- Images are downscaled to at most 1280 px on the long side and JPEG-encoded at quality 70 in memory (bytes). The
  `Capture` object never writes files.
- `FakeWindowSource` and `FakeGrabber` exist for tests.

### 4.2 Glance policy (`riya/core/eyes/policy.py`, pure)
A glance is allowed only when **all** of these hold:
- Eyes are enabled.
- Not in "don't look" mode.
- Presence is focused or talking (not away, break or gaming).
- The window isn't minimized, isn't Riya, and isn't denylisted.
- The online model is available.
- The daily cap isn't reached (default 150 glances a day; the counter resets at local midnight).
- At least `min_gap_s` (default 45 s) since the last glance.

A glance is **wanted** when any of these happens:
- The foreground window changed (hwnd or title) and has been stable for 4 s.
- 5 minutes have passed in the same window and its small grayscale thumbnail (64×36) differs from the last glance's by
  a mean absolute difference above 6/255.

Screen questions bypass `min_gap_s` but not the privacy checks, the cap or "don't look".

Default denylist, as case-insensitive regexes:
- Title: `password|1password|bitwarden|keepass|lastpass|bank|banking|paypal|upi|net ?banking|credit card|inprivate|incognito|private browsing|windows security|credential|sign in to|login|sign in|sign-in|log in|log-in`
- Process: `keepass.*|1password.*|bitwarden.*|credentialuibroker|dashlane.*|nordpass.*|enpass.*|roboform.*|keeper.*|proton ?pass.*`

### 4.3 Understanding (`riya/core/eyes/see.py`)
- **Note request** to the online model, using the image plus a fixed prompt. The reply is JSON only:
  `{"app": str, "activity": str ≤ 12 words, "details": str ≤ 30 words, "notable": str | null}`. `notable` is for errors,
  failing tests or anything she'd want to mention. Failures or unparseable output produce no note, and nothing is
  retried immediately.
- **Screen question:** the image plus her normal prompt (persona, memories, feelings) plus the question, sent through
  the online model in her voice. If the online model is unavailable she says she can't see right now, and answers from
  the window title and process only if that helps.
- Vision requests go to the OpenAI-compatible endpoint with `content` parts (`text` + `image_url`
  `data:image/jpeg;base64,…`), which the Gemini OpenAI compatibility layer supports. The default model is
  `RIYA_ONLINE_MODEL`, whose default becomes `gemini-flash-latest` in v0.2.

### 4.4 Engine integration
- Screen-question detection is a pure function. A question counts when it contains deictic screen words ("this", "that",
  "here", "my screen", "on screen", "this error", "this code", "what am I looking at", "read this", "see this", "look
  at") together with a question or imperative. False positives are acceptable because the privacy checks still apply.
- The prompt gets a `### What they're doing now:` line from the latest note (when newer than 10 min), and up to 3 recent
  notes, kept out of the memory block so she doesn't treat them as user statements.
- A bid may use the latest note as its reason ("still on that bug?"). Bids still happen only on breaks.
- Glance notes go to a new `glances` table (`id, at, app, title, activity, details, notable`). Purge happens after 7
  days. Consolidation receives notes from the chunk's time span as context.

### 4.5 Protocol and body
- Core → body:
  - `look {side: "left" | "right" | "center" | null, watching: bool}`, sent on window change and presence change.
  - `eyes {enabled: bool, dont_look: bool, glances_today: int, cap: int}`, sent on change and on hello.
- Body → core: `dont_look {on: bool}` and `glance_delete {id}`. `memories` gains `glances`.
- Settings gain these fields:
  - `eyes_enabled` (default true)
  - `glance_daily_cap` (150)
  - `glance_min_gap_s` (45)
  - `eyes_deny_titles` (list[str] of extra regexes)
  - `eyes_deny_processes` (list[str])
- Body:
  - `leanFor` uses `look.side` and `watching`.
  - Body's main process registers the don't-look hotkey with the same fallback scheme as the talk hotkey, plus a tray
    toggle.
  - While `dont_look` is on, she shows `covering_eyes`.
  - The settings page gets an "Eyes" section: toggle, cap, deny patterns, and today's count.
  - The memory page lists glance notes, each with delete.

## 5. Verification
- Pure tests for the policy table, the denylist, the screen-question detector, note parsing, the cap and midnight reset,
  and the thumbnail diff.
- A capture test on Windows grabs the foreground window into memory. It asserts JPEG bytes and no file writes, by
  patching `open` and `Image.save` to fail for any path.
- A protocol contract test and schema regeneration. Body tests for lean side and the don't-look hotkey fallback.
- A live check, `evaluation/eyes_check.py`. With an online key it glances once at a test window (Notepad, opened by the
  script with known text) and asks "what does this say?". Without a key it reports "skipped (no online key)".

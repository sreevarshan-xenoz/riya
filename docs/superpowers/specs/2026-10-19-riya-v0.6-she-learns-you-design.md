# Riya v0.6: "She learns you"

Design document, 2026-10-19. Status: approved on autopilot. The user delegated the decisions: "continue … until we make the full polished riya".

## 1. Intent

The user wants Riya to feel like a real companion: sweet, a little teasing, clingy, and smart like Jarvis. A companion who never learns is a chatbot with a face. The v0.1 design lists "personality learning from feedback" and "learning the user's decisions (the decision twin)" for Phase 4.

v0.6 is the first slice. Riya:
- notices how the user reacts to her;
- remembers how they like to be talked to;
- slowly adjusts her personality dials within her character;
- learns when the user actually wants her to start a conversation, offer a game or speak a report, and when they don't.

Everything she learns is visible, explainable and resettable in settings.

`riya/core/traits.json` already holds versioned trait values (sweetness, teasing, clinginess, verbosity) with a change history. v0.6 makes those values live.

## 2. Goals
1. **Explicit feedback.**
   - When the user tells her how to be, she remembers it as a style note. Examples: "stop teasing me", "you can tease more", "shorter answers please", "I like it when you ask about my day", "don't use emojis", "talk normally", "be more cute". The note is in her own words, plus a dial nudge.
   - She acknowledges with a short templated line in her voice ("Okay~ less teasing, got it."). She never argues.
2. **Implicit signals.** These are logged as events: bid answered or ignored, game offer accepted or declined, report heard or barged in on, a reply interrupted within 2 s, and the user's estimated mood change after her line (the existing user-mood estimate). They feed the decision twin. Except for a slow verbosity drift (§4.3), they never move dials by themselves.
3. **Personality dials.**
   - The dials are sweetness, teasing, clinginess, verbosity and emoji. Each one is in [0, 1] and clamped to a character band:
     - sweetness 0.6–1.0
     - teasing 0.1–0.8
     - clinginess 0.4–1.0
     - verbosity 0.15–0.7
     - emoji 0.0–0.6
   - The bands keep her Riya. She can be less teasing but never cold, and never more clingy than her design.
   - Dials shape the prompt (a short style line) and shaping (the sentence caps scale with verbosity, and the emoji limit follows emoji).
   - Every change is appended to the traits history, with the date, the reason and the source (explicit or drift).
4. **Decision twin, first slice.**
   - For each kind of her initiative (break bid, game offer, finished-agent report and greeting length), she keeps a small model of whether the user engages. It's bucketed by context: hour band (night, morning, afternoon, evening), presence state, and work stretch (short or long).
   - The model is Beta(α, β) counts per bucket with a prior. Bids and offers in buckets the user reliably ignores become rarer. The probability floor is 0.15, so she never goes fully silent: clinginess is part of who she is. Buckets the user enjoys keep their normal rate.
   - Greeting length follows engagement with greetings.
   - Urgent agent reports (failed, waiting) and reminders are never suppressed. The twin only shapes the optional things.
5. **"How they like me" block.** Up to 5 most recent active style notes go into the prompt as a `### How they like you:` block, kept outside the memory block. They are not facts and never go to consolidation.
6. **Settings page "She learns you".**
   - The dials, shown as read-only bars with their band, plus their history.
   - The style notes, each with delete.
   - The twin's buckets: engagement rate and count per initiative kind, with a reset per kind.
   - A master toggle `learning_enabled`, default on. When it's off, everything is frozen as is.
   - "Reset to her defaults", which restores the v0.1 traits, clears the notes and resets the twin. It asks for confirmation in the body.

## 3. Non-goals and anti-goals
- **Non-goals:**
  - fine-tuning the model (the Riya v1 LoRA is a separate, later item);
  - learning facts about the world;
  - predicting the user's decisions on their own tasks (that's a later decision-twin phase);
  - multi-user.
- **Anti-goals:**
  - **Sycophancy drift.** She becomes a yes-machine, or her sweetness and teasing collapse to bland. The bands and the "explicit feedback only" rule for dials prevent this.
  - **Learning from text that isn't the user's own turn.** Screen notes, agent payloads, tool output, web text and her own lines never become style notes or move dials.
  - **Hidden change.** Anything learned that the user can't see or undo in settings.
  - **Silence.** The twin suppressing urgent reports or reminders, or pushing optional initiative to zero.
  - **Online leakage.** Style notes or dials sent online beyond the persona line that already goes there.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Feedback intents (`riya/core/learn/feedback.py`, pure)
- `detect_feedback(text) -> Feedback | None`. Deterministic patterns over the user's turn.
- `Feedback` is `(dial: str | None, delta: float, note: str | None)`.
  - **Dial phrases:**
    - teasing: "stop/less teasing", "tease me more", "you can tease (more)"
    - verbosity: "shorter/longer answers", "talk less/more", "too long"
    - emoji: "no/fewer emojis", "use more emojis"
    - sweetness: "be sweeter/cuter", "less cutesy", "talk normally"
    - clinginess: "you're too clingy", "I like that you miss me"
  - **Deltas:** ±0.15 per explicit request, then clamped to the band.
  - **Style-note phrases:** "I like it when you …", "I don't like it when you …", "please don't …", "please always …". The note is stored in third person ("They like it when I ask about their day"), using the existing `third_person` normaliser.
- Negations, questions about her ("do you like teasing?") and narration ("she said stop teasing") don't count.
- Feedback detection runs on the user's own turn only, after the existing priority intents. It is skipped mid-game and while a confirmation is pending. A turn can both carry feedback and get a normal reply: the acknowledgement is prepended as one templated line.

### 4.2 Learning store (schema v4)
- `traits` stay in KV, initialised from traits.json. Each change appends `{at, dial, old, new, reason, source}` to a `trait_history` table.
- `style_notes(id, at, text, polarity like|dislike|rule, active)`, capped at 30 active. Delete is a real delete.
- `twin(kind, bucket, alpha, beta, updated_at)`. The prior is α=2, β=2. Old evidence decays with a half-life of 30 days, applied on update.
- `events(id, at, kind, outcome, context_json)` is a raw signal log for explainability. It keeps 30 days.
- Migration v3 → v4 follows the v0.5 pattern: a one-time backup, then refuse a future version.

### 4.3 Dials in effect
- **Prompt.** One style line is built from the dials by a template. Examples: teasing low → "Keep teasing very light."; verbosity low → "Keep it short." It is added to both the local and the online persona. Dials are never sent anywhere else.
- **Shaping.** The sentence caps for small, about_me and greeting scale with verbosity, from −1 to +1 sentence. The emoji limit is 0 when emoji < 0.15 and 1 otherwise.
- **Verbosity drift.** If, in 10 consecutive replies, the user barges in within 2 s at least 5 times, verbosity drifts down by 0.05. This applies at most once a day and is logged with source "drift". It's the only implicit dial change.

### 4.4 Decision twin (`riya/core/learn/twin.py`, pure)
- `bucket(now, tz, presence, work_stretch_s) -> str`.
- `record(kind, bucket, engaged: bool)`.
- `p_engage(kind, bucket) -> float` is the posterior mean with decay.
- `should(kind, bucket, rng) -> bool`. It fires with probability max(0.15, p) for bids and game offers.
- `greet_style(bucket) -> "short" | "normal"`. It is short when p < 0.4.
- **Engagement is defined per kind:**
  - Bid: the user replies within BID_IGNORED_S (the existing rule).
  - Game offer: accepted.
  - Finished-agent report: not barged in on within 2 s.
  - Greeting: the user's next turn comes within 120 s.
- The engine consults `should` in `_maybe_bid` and for game offers, and `greet_style` in `_came_back`. Urgent paths never consult the twin.

### 4.5 Protocol and body
- **Core → body:** `learning {dials: {name: {value, lo, hi}}, history: [...last 20], notes: [...], twin: [{kind, bucket, p, n}], enabled}`. Sent on change, on hello, and when settings are opened.
- **Body → core:** `note_delete_style {id}`, `twin_reset {kind}`, `learning_reset {}` (the body confirms first), and `set_settings {learning_enabled}`.
- **Body:** a "She learns you" settings section as described in §2.6. All core-derived text is rendered as textContent only (the v0.4 rule).

## 5. Verification
- **Pure tests:**
  - feedback patterns: positives, negatives, questions, narration, third-person notes;
  - band clamping;
  - twin math: prior, decay, floor, buckets across DST;
  - shaping caps scaled by verbosity;
  - the style-line templates.
- **Engine tests (FakeModel):**
  - "stop teasing" lowers teasing, acknowledges, shows up in history, and changes the prompt's style line;
  - a style note appears in the `### How they like you:` block and never reaches consolidation or the online request beyond the persona line;
  - an injected "stop teasing" inside a screen note or an agent payload changes nothing;
  - ignored bids in a bucket reduce bids there, but never below the floor;
  - urgent reports are unaffected by the twin;
  - with learning off, nothing changes;
  - reset restores the v0.1 traits.
- **Migration test** from a populated v3 DB.
- **Body tests:** the settings section rendering, the delete, reset and twin-reset messages, and the HTML-sink guard.
- **Persona scorecard:** `evaluation/persona_eval.py` gains a `--dials` option to run the probes at low and high teasing and verbosity. This shows the dials change the style without breaking character (the existing metrics stay within ±15 pts noise).

# Riya v0.11: "She uses what she knows" (memory that reaches personal requests)

Design document. 2026-10-21. Status: approved on autopilot.

## 1. Intent and why

The user's first ask was that Riya feels like she really knows them. Today she has the facts but doesn't see them when
the user asks for something personal. `Memory.retrieve` shows a fact only when:
- its bge-small cosine is ≥ 0.80;
- or it is ≥ 0.60 and shares a content word with the query;
- or the query is an explicit about-me question (`about_me()`), which fills up to 8 facts.

Applied requests are written in other words than the facts, so they fail all three.

**Evidence** (2026-10-21, the live `FastEmbedder`, measured on retrieval only; no model replied):

| What | Result |
|---|---|
| The 30 about-me probes, each in its own conversation's memory (the gate's setting) | 0 retrieve a fact by similarity, under `HashEmbedder` and under bge |
| Relevant facts on the applied and topical probes | Cosines 0.34–0.70 (snack→popcorn 0.52, playlist→jazz piano 0.59, dessert→lactose 0.51) |
| Near-miss facts on the unknown probes (brother known, sister asked) | 0.35–0.61: no cosine floor separates them from relevant facts |
| bge's query instruction ("Represent this sentence for searching…") | Lowers every cosine by 0.03–0.08 and improves no rank |
| Topic tags from embedding prototypes | Misfiles facts: hamster → food, car → family, the Riya project → pets |
| Prototype of §4.1–4.2 (detector, floor 0.45, at most 3 extra facts) | Fires on 17 of 27 `g_about` probes and on 0 of 30 unknown, 30 AI, 9 small-talk, 4 explain and 2 remember probes. A relevant fact reaches the prompt on 16 of the 17 (from 0), with 1 irrelevant fact dragged in. Non-personal retrieval is unchanged. |
| The same prototype, one pooled memory of all 64 probe facts | Relevant fact present on 9 of 17, with 2.4 irrelevant facts per turn: bge-small's ranking is the limit |
| Switching the harness to bge (v0.10.1 rules) | One unknown probe gains a near-miss fact. "what's my neighbour's dog called?" pulls "best friend … is called Peregrine" (0.60, sharing only "called"), and live Riya does the same today. |

**The gate is blind to all of this.** `persona_eval` runs her core with `HashEmbedder`, but live Riya uses
`FastEmbedder` (`riya/core/__main__.py: make_embedder`). In the v0.10.1 bank, base about-me recall was about 0% on 27 of
30 probes. The about-me plant was then caught only 82% of the time (the floor is 90%), because there was no recall left
to regress.

## 2. Goals

1. **She uses what she knows.** On a personal request, relevant facts reach her prompt with no shared words. A personal
   request is either:
   - **applied:** "pick … for me", "you know what I like", "what should I eat/watch/order";
   - **topical:** "what do you know about my family", "describe my habits".

   The mechanism is a deterministic detector plus a bounded, lower-floor fill (§4.1, §4.2).
2. **She doesn't drag in irrelevant facts.** The fill adds at most 3 facts, keeps the per-turn total at 5, and runs only
   on personal requests. "Irrelevant fact used" is measured at two levels: retrieval (deterministic) and reply
   (informational) (§4.4).
3. **The gate measures the live behaviour.** `persona_eval` uses the live embedder, pinned and verified. Its identity
   joins `gate.identity()` (§4.5).
4. **Honesty is untouched.** The detector never fires on the unknown or AI banks. Non-personal retrieval is unchanged
   except for one stricter fix, the naming word (§4.3).

## 3. Non-goals, anti-goals and constraints

**Non-goals:**
- New memory types, schema changes or fact tags.
- Cloud services.
- Training changes.
- Gate statistics: α, B, the seed, the repeat, the tests, the targets, the vetoes, the adopt rules and the calibration
  criteria all stay.
- Changing `about_me_recalls` or any other gate scorer's definition.
- **Direct attribute questions in other words** ("what did i name my kitty?" against "cat … Pistachio"). These stay
  strict in v0.11. They are where near-miss facts become guessed values (§1: the cosines overlap). They are parked for
  a local reranker (§6).

**Anti-goals:**
- **Goalpost moving.** This version changes the product (her core, which both arms of the gate share) and makes the
  harness match live Riya. It does not touch the gate's bar. The about-me plant keeps j = 6 and ≥ 90%. Every
  calibration criterion stays as pre-registered in v0.10 §4.6 and the v0.10.1 rulings. The design used base-side and
  retrieval-only data. No candidate transcript was read.
- **Tuning on the gate's probes.** The detector and the constants are built and checked on a separate dev set (§4.6).
  The probe bank is a held-out report.
- **Honesty harm.** No new fact may reach the prompt of a probe in the unknown or AI banks.
- **Noise in the prompt.** Never more than 3 added facts, and never more than 5 facts in a non-about-me turn.
- **Dragged facts going online.** The fill runs only on the local route, like the likes notes.

**Constraints:**
- **Local and free.** fastembed runs on onnxruntime on the CPU, so the local GPU is untouched. The fill reuses the
  cosines `retrieve` already computes, so there are no extra embedding calls.
- **Tests.** Tests use recorded vectors and never download. They never touch the real `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Riya` or
  `C:\Users\sreev\models` (the existing `tests/conftest.py` guards stay) and never run the hooks installer.
- **Prompt budget.** A personal turn adds at most 3 fact lines (about 15 tokens each) and one situation line of at most
  40 tokens. That fits the 16k context, and the bound keeps it readable for the 1.2B model.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The personal-request detector (`riya/core/memory.py`)

`personal_request(query: str) -> bool` is pure regex, next to `about_me()`. It returns False whenever `about_me(query)`
is True, so the v0.9 path wins.

**Fires on:**
- **Applied:** an action verb (pick, choose, suggest, recommend, plan, make, find) with "for me" or "me" as the object.
- "you know what I like", "you know how I am", "you know my taste".
- "what should I eat / get / order / watch / listen to / wear / cook / drink / ask (them) for".
- "something/anything/that I'd (actually) like/enjoy/love".
- "very me", "so me", "what can't I eat".
- **Topical:** "what do/did you (actually) know/remember about my … / where I … / how I …".
- "what have you figured out / picked up / learned / noticed about me/my/them".
- "describe me/my …", "what kind of person am I".

**Never fires on:**
- Direct attribute questions ("what's / who's / when's / how old is my …", "what did I name my …").
- Shared-history questions ("what did I tell/say/mention …", "remember when I told you …").
- Leading questions ("you know my cat's name, right?", "do you (happen to) know my …").
- Identity questions, small talk with "we" ("what should we do tonight?"), and remember-that turns.

**Why a detector:** it's deterministic, unit-testable and auditable, and the form of a request is a better signal than
cosine. The forms that ask her to apply what she knows are exactly the ones where a dragged-in fact can't become a
guessed value. The alternatives were rejected on measurements (§1):

| Candidate | Rejected because |
|---|---|
| Top-k for every first-person query | "my" is in all 30 unknown probes. It would put near-miss facts into exactly the questions where guessing is the failure, and no floor separates them. |
| Fact tags matched to request types | Embedding prototypes misfile facts. A lexicon is a large hand-tuned table and a schema change (a non-goal). |
| Query expansion | bge's instruction prefix helps no rank. LLM expansion costs a 1.2B call per turn and is not deterministic. |

### 4.2 The personal fill (`Memory.retrieve`)

`retrieve(query, …, personal: bool = True)`. When `personal`, `personal_request(query)`, and the embedder defines
`personal_sim`:
1. Facts that pass `eligible` come first, ranked as now.
2. Up to `PERSONAL_EXTRA = 3` more live facts are added, each with cosine ≥ `personal_sim` and ranked by `_score`. The
   total stays ≤ `k_facts` (5).
3. `Retrieved` gains `filled: tuple[int, ...] = ()`, the ids the fill added. It is used for the situation line and for
   the retrieval-level check.

Episodes and threads are unchanged.

**Constants:**
- `FastEmbedder.personal_sim = 0.45`. It keeps every relevant fact measured on the applied probes, and at least one
  on every topical probe except `g_about_24` (0.445 and 0.41). It drops 3 of the 4 distractors. `PERSONAL_EXTRA`, not
  the floor, bounds the count.
- `HashEmbedder` has no `personal_sim`, so it gets no fill. Its cosine for a paraphrase is 0, so a fill would be
  recency noise. The degraded fallback keeps v0.10.1 behaviour.

**Unchanged otherwise:** when `personal_request` is False, `about_me` is True, or `personal=False`, the output is
identical to v0.10.1 plus §4.3.

### 4.3 One stricter fix: naming words aren't shared content

`eligible`'s shared-word check ignores `NAMING = {"call", "calls", "called", "name", "names", "named"}`. Those words
say nothing about the topic, and today they let "…dog called?" retrieve "…friend is called Peregrine" (§1).
`STOPWORDS`, `content_tokens`, `HashEmbedder` and dedup are untouched, which keeps the blast radius small.

### 4.4 The prompt and "irrelevant fact used"

**Engine.**
- `_retrieve(query, threads, personal=route != "online")`. The route is already decided before retrieval
  (engine.py:637), so dragged facts never go online.
- On a reply whose query is a personal request and whose memory block holds ≥ 1 fact, the situation gains
  `personal_situation()`. It is one line of ≤ 40 tokens, for example: "They want you to use what you know about them:
  draw only on the memories above that fit, and if none fit, say you don't know that about them yet."
- The turn kind is unchanged (small or explain). A personal turn is not an about-me turn, so there is no "list the
  block" rule and no denial-proof situation.
- `prompt_fingerprint()`'s canary includes the new situation, as for every situation builder.

**Retrieval level (deterministic, offline): `evaluation/retrieval_eval.py --personal`.** Over the dev set (§4.6) it
reports:
- `personal_recall`: a relevant fact is in `facts`. Measured per question (its own small memory, like the gate) and
  pooled (the whole dev background).
- `dragged_mean` and `dragged_max`: added facts not marked relevant, per personal turn.
- `nonpersonal_identical`: the share of non-personal questions whose retrieval is unchanged with the fill on or off.

The same report runs over the probe bank, from recorded vectors.

**Reply level (informational): `style_metrics.irrelevant_fact_used(reply, relevant, others, user_text)`.** It is True
when the reply contains a content word (`_content`, minus `NOT_RECALL`) of a non-relevant fact that occurs in no
relevant fact and not in the user's text.
- About-me probes with a distractor gain `"relevant": [fact indices]`; when it is absent, every fact is relevant.
- `persona_eval` prints the share on its own line.
- It is not in `row_outcomes`, `WEAK`, `INFO` or the targets, so the gate's rules are untouched. It makes visible any
  recall that `about_me_recalls` credits to a distractor.

### 4.5 The gate measures the live embedder

**Pin** (`riya/core/embed.py`). `EMBEDDER_PIN` covers:
- the model `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, which fastembed serves as `Qdrant/bge-small-en-v1.5-onnx-Q`, snapshot
  `aa8f8b06…`;
- `model_optimized.onnx` at 66,465,124 bytes, sha256 `51f1bd0a…` (the builder copies the full hashes from the cache);
- the tokenizer file hashes;
- `fastembed==0.9.0`.

`FastEmbedder.identity()` hashes the loaded snapshot's files. `FastEmbedder(verify=True)` raises on a mismatch with the
pin. Live Riya only logs a warning, so an upstream re-upload can't break her memory.
`requirements-riya.txt` pins `fastembed==0.9.0`.

**`persona_eval`.**
- `--embedder {auto,fast,hash}`, default `auto`: `fast` with `verify=True` when it loads, otherwise `hash` with a
  printed note.
- The embedder is built once per run and passed to every `run_conversation`. Loading takes about 1 s; embedding adds
  about 10 ms per turn.
- The transcript header and the score record `embedder_id`.
- `RIYA_MODEL_DIR` chooses the cache. The default is the live `model_dir()`, so the laptop never downloads it twice.

**`gate`.**
- `IDENTITY_FIELDS` gains `"embedder"`. `identity()["embedder"]` is the pin string, a code constant, so an adopt on the
  laptop needs no model files.
- A verdict's runs must carry `embedder_id == EMBEDDER_PIN` (a verdict check). `calibration_problem` refuses a record
  for another embedder.
- `GATE_VERSION = "0.11.0"`, because the scorer files and the probes changed. `rules_fingerprint`'s pin moves with the
  version only. This is stricter: an old record or verdict can't match.

**Kaggle and Colab.**
- `pip install fastembed==0.9.0`, then `RIYA_MODEL_DIR=/tmp/riya/models` on Kaggle or `/content/drive/MyDrive/riya_v1/models`
  on Colab. The first `FastEmbedder()` downloads about 66 MB from HF; it is not 130 MB.
- Offline fallback: add the HF cache folder `models--Qdrant--bge-small-en-v1.5-onnx-Q` to the `riya-gate-inputs`
  dataset (or Drive) and copy it into `RIYA_MODEL_DIR` first.
- `verify=True` proves the bytes either way. `run_gate.py` prints `gate.identity()` and the verified `embedder_id`
  before the first run.

### 4.6 Tuning discipline: a dev set, and the bank as a held-out report

**`tests/data/personal_set.json`** is a new file. The v0.1 `retrieval_set.json` stays frozen as the v0.9 regression.
It holds:
- ≥ 30 background facts;
- ≥ 24 personal requests (≥ 14 applied, ≥ 10 topical). Each has its relevant fact ids and its own small memory
  (relevant facts plus ≥ 1 distractor on at least half);
- ≥ 20 negatives: direct attribute, shared-history, leading, near-miss, small talk with "my", and "what should we …".

It is written independently of `persona_probes.json`. It shares no 6-word normalised window with the bank (reuse
`probe_holdout`'s normaliser), and no fact values. The detector's patterns are written from the dev set.

**Recorded vectors:** `tests/data/bge_vectors.npz` (float32) holds every dev-set and probe-bank text with its
`embedder_id`. `RecordedEmbedder` carries FastEmbedder's thresholds and raises on an unknown text. An opt-in test, run
only with `RIYA_TEST_BGE_CACHE` set to an existing cache, read-only, re-embeds the texts and checks them to 1e-4.

**The constants (0.45, 3, 5) are fixed here,** before any model answers a probe under v0.11. If the dev set disagrees
(A3), the floor may move only within {0.40, 0.45, 0.50}, on dev evidence, as a ruling, and before the Kaggle step.

**Why the bank can't be gamed.** Retrieval is shared by both arms, so a detector that happens to fit the bank raises
both arms equally and can't favour a candidate. It would only flatter the absolute recall. That is why the dev set, not
the bank, carries the live claim.


### 4.7 Builder-check decisions (Claude's calls, 2026-10-21)

1. **A4 is a report, not a blocker.**
   - Its denominator is the fixed 17 personal `g_about` probes.
   - The real bar is A16's pre-registered about-me plant.
   - The dev set and the detector are frozen before the bank is looked at; nothing is added to the dev set after a bank miss.
   - Only one change may follow the bank: if the detector fires on a bank unknown or AI probe, it may be tightened (the safe direction), and the change is ledgered.
2. **The gate identity covers retrieval.** `riya/core/memory.py` joins `gate.SCORER_FILES`. That is stricter, and any detector or floor change invalidates calibration records and verdicts.
3. **No test downloads anything.**
   - `test_retrieval_set_with_bge` runs only when `RIYA_TEST_BGE_CACHE` names an existing cache, and skips otherwise. It never downloads.
   - The recorded vectors cover the default run.
   - A7's "pass unchanged" means the hash-embedder tests.
   - The controller deletes the copy the old test downloaded under pytest's temp folder.
4. **The bge cache.**
   - The builder may read the existing `%TEMP%astembed_cache` model read-only, to build `bge_vectors.npz` and copy the pin's hashes. It does this from a copy in a tmp dir, never the original, so fastembed can't write into it.
   - The live app keeps `model_dir()`. A first live run downloads about 66 MB once, which is a user-visible note in the README.
5. **`embedder_id` travels in the verdict.** It is written by `persona_eval.run`, then carried through `verdict.REQUIRED`, `make_verdict`, `champion` and `gate_calibrate`, and checked against `identity()["embedder"]`. When `--embedder auto` falls back to hash in a bank or verdict run, `champion` refuses up front with exit 2.
6. **SFT data stays out of scope.** `build_sft` is unchanged, since training changes are a non-goal. A guard test proves that no SFT scenario matches `personal_request`; the `scenarios.py:262` near-match is checked explicitly.
7. **A separate, blind subagent writes the dev set.** It doesn't see the detector, and the set is committed before the detector task starts.
8. **The fill applies only to `kind == "reply"` on the local route.** It never applies to tool lines, greetings, bids, reports or games.
9. **Confirmed: online-routed requests get no fill.** For example, `router.THINKING` matches "plan", so those requests go online and get no personal fill. This is documented as a known limit, with no route change.
10. **How `irrelevant_fact_used` is scored.**
    - It covers only the rows that carry a `relevant` annotation in the probe file.
    - `persona_eval` copies `relevant` onto the transcript rows, so a verdict can be re-scored.
    - The denominator is the annotated rows.
    - `remember_aboutme` is excluded.
11. **If A16 fails, the v0.10 §3 and §4.6.2 remedies still govern.** The repeat remedy is already spent. What remains is more measurement (more probes or more live runs) or a scorer fix backed by quoted transcripts.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

All offline and CPU-only, with recorded vectors unless noted.

**Detector:**
- **A1** `personal_request` fires on ≥ 90% of the dev set's personal requests and on 0 of its negatives.
- **A2** On the probe bank it fires on 0 of these: the 30 unknown, 20 AI, 10 off-path AI, small, explain, remember and
  greeting turns. Its hits on the about-me probes are reported, and §1's prototype found 17 of 27.

**She uses what she knows:**
- **A3** Dev `personal_recall` per question ≥ 80% and pooled ≥ 50% (the pooled figure is reported against §1's 9/17).
- **A4** Probe bank, gate setting: a relevant fact is in the prompt on ≥ 15 of the personal `g_about` probes (0 today,
  16 in the prototype).

**No dragging:**
- **A5** Every personal turn adds ≤ 3 facts and holds ≤ 5. This is a property test over the dev set, the bank and
  random memories.
- **A6** Dev `dragged_mean` is reported and ≤ 1.0 per question and ≤ 2.5 pooled. Bank `dragged` total ≤ 2 (1 in the
  prototype).

**v0.9 about-me unchanged:**
- **A7** The existing `test_memory_recall`, `test_retrieval_set` and `test_prompt` tests pass unchanged.
- **A8** `nonpersonal_identical = 1.0` on the dev set and the bank. The only change outside personal turns is §4.3,
  pinned by its own test.
- **A9** The explicit about-me probes still get the 8-fact fill.

**Honesty:**
- **A10** With recorded bge vectors and §4.3, every unknown and AI probe's prompt holds no fact. That includes
  `g_unknown_24`, which carries one today.
- **A11** An online-routed turn gets no fill and no personal situation (engine test with fakes).

**Prompt:**
- **A12** The added text on a personal turn (fact lines plus the situation) is ≤ 600 characters for the bank's longest
  facts. `prompt_fingerprint()` changes and its test pin is updated.

**Harness and identity:**
- **A13**
  - `persona_eval --embedder auto` uses a fake fast embedder when it loads and hash otherwise, and records
    `embedder_id`.
  - One embedder is built per run.
  - `gate.identity()` has `embedder`.
  - `calibration_problem` refuses an embedder mismatch, and a verdict check refuses runs with another `embedder_id`.
  - GATE_VERSION is 0.11.0.
- **A14** `FastEmbedder(verify=True)` raises on a tampered fake snapshot and passes on a matching one. Live mode only
  warns. No test downloads.

**Reply-level check:**
- **A15** `irrelevant_fact_used` has pure unit tests: a distractor's word flags the reply; a relevant fact's word, or a
  word also in the user's text, doesn't.

**Controller's Kaggle step (not a code task), after merge:**
- **A16** A fresh v0.11 calibration: 5 live base-vs-base runs at repeat 4. v0.10 §4.6.5 says new prompts need new live
  runs, so the v0.10.1 bank isn't re-scored. It must pass the unchanged criteria:
  - A1 has ≤ 1 false decision out of 5;
  - A2 is ≤ 6%;
  - the AI, unknown and invented plants are caught as before;
  - the about-me j = 6 plant is caught ≥ 90%.

  The report includes base about-me recall on the personal probes (expected well above 0), base unknown honesty next to
  the v0.10.1 bank's, and the `irrelevant_fact_used` share.

  If the about-me plant still fails, that is a product finding (she sees the facts but doesn't use them). Its remedy is
  a v0.11.x prompt ruling or v0.10 §3's measurement remedies, never a weaker plant.

**Eligibility.** Under v0.11, round 2 is eligible as in v0.10 §4.6.1: §3's rule concerns scoring under v0.11 itself.

## 6. User-only steps

- **A read for feel.** After A16, read about ten personal-request replies from the bank (or ask her live: "pick a snack
  for me"). Does she use the fact naturally, and does she bring up odd ones? The metrics measure presence, not charm.
- **Parked, the user's call later:** direct paraphrased questions ("what did i name my kitty?") need something smarter
  than bge-small. The candidate is a local cross-encoder reranker, CPU-only, a larger download, with some added latency
  per turn. It is not in v0.11.
- Nothing to install: fastembed is already a dependency, and live Riya already downloads bge on first run.

# Riya v0.12 "Morpheus in Riya" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** She keeps four small predictors of things she can check (when they come back, whether they answer, how long an
agent run takes, when they game). Surprise and confidence from a ported MORPHEUS appraisal move her feelings, and every
statement about the user's routine is an engine-made claim or hunch, logged when said and audited later into a visible
honesty rate. With the switch off she is exactly v0.11.

**Architecture:**
- **A pure package `riya/core/routine/`:**
  - `appraisal.py`: the ported formulas (surprise, robust scale, startle, CUSUM with softening, confidence).
  - `predictors.py`: `LogNormalCell` (Normal-Gamma on log seconds), `BetaCell`, the Student-t and Beta helpers.
  - `statements.py`: kinds, evidence rules, templates, the volume and clinginess caps.
  - `detect.py`: `claim_guard`, `routine_question`, `goodbye`, `disputes`.
  - `records.py`: `RoutineDB` on schema v6, behind the memory lock.
  - `audit.py`: per-kind scoring, the two readings, the tally and the honesty numbers.
  - `__init__.py`: the `Routine` facade the engine calls.
- **Sensors:** `AgentHub` measures `run_s`, and `Presence` exposes `stretch_start()` and `censored`.
- **The engine:**
  - A `_routine_step` in the tick feeds P1–P4.
  - `Feelings.on_appraisal` moves her within her bands.
  - Statements ride as leads and tails, and `claim_guard` filters model sentences.
  - Routine turns are scrubbed before any online call, and statement utterances are never consolidated.
- **The body and Health:** `RoutineMsg` and `routine_forget`, the Settings section "She notices your routines", and a
  Health row `honesty`.
- **Measurement:** `evaluation/routine_sim.py` drives the real `RiyaCore` with a fake clock over six personas. Constants are
  tuned only on dev seeds 0–19 and judged on held-out seeds 100–159, in a separate final task.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, numpy, SQLite, pydantic, pytest; the body is Electron with `node --test`. CPU only.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-riya-v0.12-morpheus-in-riya-design.md`. Its §4.12 (builder-check rulings) is
binding and wins over §1–§4.11, §5 and §6 wherever they differ. The builder-check notes are in
`scratch_runs/v0.12-builder-check.md`.

6. **The Normal-Gamma cell** (on y = ln seconds):
   - The prior is μ₀ = ln(prior median), κ₀ = 1, α₀ = 2 and β₀ = α₀·σ₀².
   - The stored, decayed sufficient statistics are `n` (= n_eff), `s1 = Σy` and `s2 = Σy²`. The posterior is the
     standard update from them.
   - The predictive is Student-t: dof 2αₙ, location μₙ, scale² = βₙ(κₙ + 1)/(αₙκₙ).
   - Decay multiplies n, s1 and s2 by 0.5^(Δt/30 d). A row whose n, s1, s2, scale, cusum or updated_at isn't finite,
     or whose n is negative, reads as the prior (the twin's corrupt-cell rule).
7. **Losses:**
   - LogNormal: z = (y − loc)/scale under the predictive before the update, and loss = 0.5·z².
   - Beta: p = the predictive mean, and loss = −ln p (outcome True) or −ln(1 − p) (False).
   - The direction comes from the sign of z, or from the outcome. P1: "early"/"late"; P3: "shorter"/"longer"; P2:
     "answered"/"ignored"; P4: "gamed"/"didnt".
8. **The appraisal follows MORPHEUS exactly where §4.3 is loose:**
   - surprise = clip(ln(ratio + 1e-12), −3, 3), with ratio = loss/(scale + floor).
   - The scale moves `scale *= 1 + k·(+1 if loss > scale else −1)`, and only on a learned observation.
   - cusum moves on every observation, learned or startled.
9. **The order of one observation:**
   1. Decay.
   2. The predictive and the appraisal values.
   3. The cusum update.
   4. If cusum > `CUSUM_H`: a change. Soften (n, s1 and s2 scaled by min(1, `SOFT_N`/n), so the mean is kept), learn the
      buffered startled values and then this one, set cusum to 0 and the buffer to empty.
   5. Else, if startled (ratio > `STARTLE_FACTOR` and n_eff ≥ `MATURE_N`): append the value to the ring of the last 3, and
      don't learn it.
   6. Else: learn it (the stats and the scale).

   `Appraisal.n_eff` and `Appraisal.confidence` are the values **before** the observation, the evidence the predictive
   stood on. `change` reports this observation's outcome.
10. **The grid (GC6, P09):**
    - A point is admissible only when `3 * (ln STARTLE_FACTOR − CUSUM_DRIFT) > CUSUM_H`.
    - `routine_sim --grid` (dev seeds only) tries admissible single moves in this order: `CLAIM_C=0.8`, `CUSUM_H=3`,
      `STARTLE_FACTOR=8`, `SURPRISE_FACTOR=3`, `SURPRISE_FACTOR=1.5`, `CLAIM_C=0.6`. Then admissible pairs, in the
      lexicographic order of that list.
    - The first point that passes every blocking dev criterion is the candidate. The controller rules it (K-dev).
    - If none passes, the defaults stand, and that is ledgered.
11. **P1:**
    - It is observed at every `came_back` event in `_greeting`, deferred or not (§4.12.10). The leave time is now −
      away_s, in the core's tz.
    - An absence is censored (not observed) when away_s < `MIN_ABSENCE_S`, when away_s > 30 days, or when the leave time
      is before the core's start-up.
    - Each observation updates both the fine cell `<band>/<weekday|weekend>` and the band cell `<band>`. The appraisal and
      the predictive come from the fine cell when its n_eff ≥ `BACKOFF_N`, else from the band cell.
12. **P3:**
    - The buckets are `claude:<folder>` (prompt-safe folder, `claude:*` when empty) and `colab:<entry>`. They back off to
      `claude:*` and `colab:*` while n_eff < `BACKOFF_N`, and both levels are updated.
    - Only `finished` or `failed` events with `run_s ≥ MIN_RUN_S` are observed.
    - A Colab run is timed from its first `running`, and stalls don't restart it. A restart means `run_s` is None.
13. **P4:**
    - A band is (local date of the band's start, band). The night band 22–05 belongs to the date it started, and its day
      type is that date's.
    - Coverage is the sum of tick gaps ≤ `STRETCH_GAP_S` inside the band. A band covered < 50% is censored.
    - "Gamed" means one continuous `gaming` span reached 600 s. It is observed True at that moment, else False at the
      band's first tick after its end, once per band.
    - The tracker is in memory, so a restart loses the current band, which is then usually censored.
14. **P2:**
    - `Routine.observe_engagement(kind, bucket, engaged)` reads `twin.p_engage(kind, bucket)` before the twin records. It
      runs only for `bid`, `game_offer` and `greeting`, only while learning is on, and from `RiyaCore._engage`.
    - Its `scale`, `cusum` and buffer live in `routine_p2`. n_eff is `twin.evidence(cell, now)`.
    - A `TwinReset(kind)` clears that kind's rows, and a `LearningReset` clears them all.
15. **"Unsure" and hysteresis (§4.12.5):**
    - A cell enters "unsure" at a change, and leaves it only after 2 consecutive observations of that cell with
      confidence ≥ 0.5 (after the update).
    - The curiosity +0.15 bump fires only when a cell enters unsure, never again while it stays unsure.
    - The state is kept in KV `routine.unsure` as `{cell: {"since", "ok"}}`.
16. **Clauses ride only on statement turns (§4.12.5):**
    - Clause 1 rides with an S2 "early" statement, clause 2 with S2 "late", and clause 4 with S8.
    - Clause 3 rides with any other statement only when a P2 "answered" appraisal with p_engage < 0.4 happened in the
      last 30 min.
    - Otherwise a statement turn has no clause.
    - The clause goes into `Feelings.prompt_tag(clause)` on the local route only.
17. **The startle face (§7):**
    - `on_appraisal` shows `surprised` for 6 s.
    - `on_return`, finding an unexpired `surprised` override, queues its own face (`Feelings._after = (name, 20)`).
      `expression()` promotes it when the surprised one expires, so it ends 20 s after that.
    - With routine off no `surprised` override ever exists, so `on_return` is unchanged.
18. **The missing bonus (P07):**
    - The bonus is `min(MISSING_BONUS_MAX, 0.15·s·clinginess/0.8)`. `on_appraisal` holds it as `Feelings._bonus = (value,
      at)`, and `on_return` adds it after its own `max()`, with missing ≤ 1.
    - A bonus older than `GREET_DEFER_MAX_S` (1800 s) is dropped unused.
    - The concern bump is `min(CONCERN_MAX, 0.15·s)`.
19. **"Overdue" (§4.4):** an absence remark needs `away_s ≥ max(p50 + OVERDUE_MIN_S, OVERDUE_P90 · p90)`, with p50 and p90
    the predictive quantiles in seconds of the cell the appraisal used.
20. **S1:**
    - t0 is `presence.stretch_start()`. The claim needs `not presence.censored`.
    - `Presence.censored` is True while the stretch began at start-up or after a clock jump back, or once any update gap
      since the stretch began exceeded `STRETCH_GAP_S`.
    - "Coding" needs ≥ 2 code-category glances with `at ≥ t0`, the newest ≤ 600 s old.
    - Bid templates are past-perfect ("you'd been at it since 9:05").
    - The audit window is (t0 + 600, w], where w is the start of the break in progress at `said_at` (the last `break`
      in `presence_log` with no `break_end` after it), else `said_at` (§4.12.3).
21. **Hunch audits and expiry:**
    - S3 is scored at the next `came_back`. It expires 24 h after it was said.
    - S6 is scored at that agent's next `finished`/`failed`, true when the remaining time r lies in
      [min(0.5g, g − 120), max(2g, g + 120)], with g the guessed remainder. It expires after 24 h.
    - The S7 bid hunch is true when that band had a gaming start that day. It is scored at the band's end.
    - S8 is true when the next 3 observations of that cell all fall outside the pre-change 50% interval (stored in its
      `value`). It expires after 28 days.
22. **Disputes and the two readings:**
    - `statements.disputed` (0/1) is its own column. A dispute is final for the user reading, and the sensor verdict is
      still computed.
    - Sensor reading: true/(true + false) over claims.
    - User reading: disputed counts false, whatever the sensor said.
    - Hunches are shown as the sensor hit rate.
    - A dispute counts only on the user's first turn after a statement, within `DISPUTE_S`.
23. **"Unknown" and routine replies:**

    - **A sentence is about the user** when it has a second-person reference (you, your, you're, you've, you'll,

      "how about", "why not", "let's", "maybe take", "try".
    - **It drops** when it holds any of these that matches nothing offered:
      - a clock time;
      - a duration (a number, or "an"/"a", with a time unit; "since <time>"; "for <n> <unit>");
      - a frequency word (always, usually, normally, typically, never, often, every, most) together with a time-of-day or
        day word.
    - **Matching:**
      - A time matches an offered time with the same hour, and the same minute when the sentence names one.
      - A duration matches an offered duration of the same number of seconds, after unit normalisation.
      - A time-of-day or day word matches the same word offered.
    - **"Offered"** is the turn's situation (statement lines included), the user's text, `prompt.offered_text(retrieved,

**Resolution 6 (inlined):** **The Normal-Gamma cell** (on y = ln seconds):
- The prior is μ₀ = ln(prior median), κ₀ = 1, α₀ = 2 and β₀ = α₀·σ₀².
- The stored, decayed sufficient statistics are `n` (= n_eff), `s1 = Σy` and `s2 = Σy²`. The posterior is the
  standard update from them.
- The predictive is Student-t: dof 2αₙ, location μₙ, scale² = βₙ(κₙ + 1)/(αₙκₙ).
- Decay multiplies n, s1 and s2 by 0.5^(Δt/30 d). A row whose n, s1, s2, scale, cusum or updated_at isn't finite,
  or whose n is negative, reads as the prior (the twin's corrupt-cell rule).

**Resolution 7 (inlined):** **Losses:**
- LogNormal: z = (y − loc)/scale under the predictive before the update, and loss = 0.5·z².
- Beta: p = the predictive mean, and loss = −ln p (outcome True) or −ln(1 − p) (False).
- The direction comes from the sign of z, or from the outcome. P1: "early"/"late"; P3: "shorter"/"longer"; P2:
  "answered"/"ignored"; P4: "gamed"/"didnt".

**Resolution 8 (inlined):** **The appraisal follows MORPHEUS exactly where §4.3 is loose:**
- surprise = clip(ln(ratio + 1e-12), −3, 3), with ratio = loss/(scale + floor).
- The scale moves `scale *= 1 + k·(+1 if loss > scale else −1)`, and only on a learned observation.
- cusum moves on every observation, learned or startled.

**Resolution 9 (inlined):** **The order of one observation:**
1. Decay.
2. The predictive and the appraisal values.
3. The cusum update.
4. If cusum > `CUSUM_H`: a change. Soften (n, s1 and s2 scaled by min(1, `SOFT_N`/n), so the mean is kept), learn the
   buffered startled values and then this one, set cusum to 0 and the buffer to empty.
5. Else, if startled (ratio > `STARTLE_FACTOR` and n_eff ≥ `MATURE_N`): append the value to the ring of the last 3, and
   don't learn it.
6. Else: learn it (the stats and the scale).

`Appraisal.n_eff` and `Appraisal.confidence` are the values **before** the observation, the evidence the predictive
stood on. `change` reports this observation's outcome.

**Resolution 6 (inlined):** **The Normal-Gamma cell** (on y = ln seconds):
- The prior is μ₀ = ln(prior median), κ₀ = 1, α₀ = 2 and β₀ = α₀·σ₀².
- The stored, decayed sufficient statistics are `n` (= n_eff), `s1 = Σy` and `s2 = Σy²`. The posterior is the
  standard update from them.
- The predictive is Student-t: dof 2αₙ, location μₙ, scale² = βₙ(κₙ + 1)/(αₙκₙ).
- Decay multiplies n, s1 and s2 by 0.5^(Δt/30 d). A row whose n, s1, s2, scale, cusum or updated_at isn't finite,
  or whose n is negative, reads as the prior (the twin's corrupt-cell rule).

# Riya v0.6 "She learns you" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Riya learns how the user likes to be talked to (explicit feedback moves her personality dials and leaves
style notes), learns when they want her optional initiative (a decision twin over bids, game offers, agent news and
greeting length), and shows all of it, resettable, in a "She learns you" settings section.

**Architecture:**
- A new package `riya/core/learn/`:
  - `dials.py`: the five dials, their bands and `clamp` (pure).
  - `feedback.py`: `detect_feedback` and the templated acks (pure).
  - `twin.py`: buckets, Beta cells with decay, the gate and the greeting style (pure; storage behind a protocol).
  - `records.py`: `LearnDB`, the schema v4 tables plus the live dials in KV.
- The engine reads the live dials (Feelings through a getter, the persona style line, the shaping caps and the emoji
  limit), detects feedback on the user's own turn after every actionable intent, gates bids and game offers through
  the twin, records engagement and barge-ins, drifts verbosity, and serves the `learning` message.
- The body gets a "She learns you" settings section; `persona_eval` gains `--dials`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12 stdlib (`sqlite3`, `zoneinfo`, `collections.deque`), pydantic, pytest; Electron with
`node --test` and Ajv.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-riya-v0.6-she-learns-you-design.md` (§4.6 is binding).

## Global Constraints
- **Dials and bands** (each in [0, 1], clamped to its band): sweetness 0.6–1.0, teasing 0.1–0.8, clinginess 0.4–1.0,
  verbosity 0.15–0.7, emoji 0.0–0.6. `clamp` rounds to 3 decimals.
- **Her defaults** are the traits.json v2 values: sweetness 0.85, teasing 0.5, clinginess 0.8, verbosity 0.35,
  emoji 0.3. "Reset to her defaults" restores exactly these.
- **Explicit feedback:** ±0.15 per request, then clamped. It counts only when the turn is ≤ 10 words or starts with
  the pattern. Negations, questions about her and narration never count. Acks are templates; she never argues.
- **Only explicit feedback moves dials**, plus one implicit rule, the verbosity drift: when the user barges in within
  2 s on at least 5 of her last 10 replies, verbosity −0.05, at most once a day, logged with source "drift".
- **Style notes:** at most 30 active; up to the 5 most recent go into the prompt as `### How they like you:`, outside
  the memory block, on the local model only. Notes never reach consolidation, facts or anything online.
- **Style line:** one templated line from the dials, appended to both the local and the online persona. Dials are
  never sent anywhere else.
- **Shaping:** the caps for small, about_me and greeting move by −1 (verbosity < 0.25) or +1 (verbosity > 0.45)
  sentence, never below 1. The emoji limit is 0 when emoji < 0.15, else 1.
- **Decision twin:**
  - Kinds `bid`, `game_offer`, `report`, `greeting`. Buckets are `"<band>/<presence>/<short|long>"`. Bands (the core's
    tz): night 22:00–04:59, morning 05:00–11:59, afternoon 12:00–16:59, evening 17:00–21:59. Long means
    `work_stretch_s > LONG_STRETCH_S` (7200).
  - Each cell is Beta(α, β) with the prior α = β = 2. Evidence above the prior decays with a 30-day half-life,
    applied on update and on read.
  - `should` fires with probability `max(0.15, min(1, p / 0.5))`: a fresh or enjoyed bucket keeps its normal rate, an
    ignored one falls toward the 0.15 floor. One roll per opportunity. A suppressed opportunity still consumes
    `initiative.last_bid_at`, is logged as event `suppressed`, and never updates the twin.
  - `greet_style` is `"short"` when p < 0.4.
  - Engagement: a bid is answered within `BID_IGNORED_S` (300 s); a game offer is accepted by `detect_start` within
    300 s; a finished-agent report is not barged in on within 2 s; a greeting gets the user's next turn within 120 s.
  - Urgent reports (failed, waiting, lost, stalled) and reminders never consult the twin. Reports are recorded and
    shown; they never gate.
- **Barge-in:** the core times the first sentence of every `_speak`. Closed (voice barge-in or a new typed turn)
  within 2 s of it is a barge-in. No protocol change.
- **Events** (`bid`, `game_offer`, `report`, `greeting`, `barge`, `mood`) are kept 30 days.
- **Learning off** (`learning_enabled`, default true): nothing changes. No detection, acks, notes, twin records,
  events or drift. What was learned still applies (frozen).
- **Own turns only:** only `reply()` (the user's typed or voice text) ever calls `detect_feedback`. Screen notes,
  agent payloads, tool output, web text, memories and her own lines never do.
- **Tests:** they never open apps, URLs or windows and never use the network (`make_core` fakes). `make_core` gives
  each core a temporary `riya_dir`; it sets `core.twin_rng = FixedRng(0.0)` from Task 6, so the twin only
  suppresses when a test says so.
- **Never run the Claude Code hooks installer.**
- **Protocol schema:** `python -m riya.core.protocol --write docs/protocol.schema.json`, never a shell redirect.
- **Commit trailer:** commit messages carry no co-author or attribution trailer.
- **Everything green stays green:** `.venv\Scripts\python -m pytest -q` and `cd body && npm test`, after every task.

## Review Focus
1. **Feedback misfires on a request.** "Give me a shorter summary of this page", "write longer answers for my essay",
   "talk less about the intro" and "there are no emojis in this doc" move no dial and get no ack. Tests: Task 1
   `test_requests_are_not_feedback`, Task 5 `test_a_request_for_a_shorter_summary_changes_nothing`.
2. **Injected text moving dials.** "stop teasing me" or "I like it when you call me boss" inside an agent payload, a
   glance note, a stored fact, a tool result or her own reply changes no dial and adds no note. Tests: Task 5
   `test_injected_feedback_never_moves_dials` and `test_only_user_turns_reach_detect_feedback`.
3. **The twin starving bids or touching urgent paths.** An ignored bucket still fires about 15% of the time, and
   urgent reports and reminders go out without consulting the twin. Tests: Task 2 `test_floor_and_chance`, Task 6
   `test_ignored_bids_make_a_bucket_rarer_but_never_below_the_floor` and
   `test_urgent_reports_and_reminders_never_consult_the_twin`.
4. **Migration v3 → v4 on a real DB.** Every v3 row survives, the backup is a readable v3 copy made once, and a
   failed migration leaves v3 untouched. Tests: Task 3 `test_a_populated_v3_db_migrates_to_v4` and
   `test_a_failed_v4_migration_leaves_the_v3_database_untouched`.
5. **Dials drifting out of band or into blandness.** Twenty "stop teasing" leave teasing at 0.1; drift stops at 0.15
   and fires once a day; the style line at every band edge keeps her character. Tests: Task 3
   `test_nudges_never_leave_the_band`, Task 4 `test_style_line_at_band_edges_stays_in_character`, Task 6
   `test_drift_is_daily_and_stops_at_the_floor`.

### Task 2: Decision twin (pure)
**Files:**
- Create: `riya/core/learn/twin.py`, `tests/learn/test_twin.py`.
- Modify: `tests/fakes.py` (adds `FixedRng`).

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `LONG_STRETCH_S` from `riya.core.initiative`.
- Produces:
  - **Constants:** `KINDS = ("bid", "game_offer", "report", "greeting")`, `PRIOR = 2.0`, `HALF_LIFE_S = 30 * 86400`,
    `FLOOR = 0.15`, `SHORT_GREET_P = 0.4`.
  - **`@dataclass(frozen=True) Cell(alpha: float, beta: float, updated_at: float)`.** The stored α and β include the
    prior.
  - **Functions:**
    - `bucket(now: float, tz, presence: str, work_stretch_s: float) -> str`, e.g. `"evening/break/long"`. The hour is
      `datetime.fromtimestamp(now, tz).hour`.
    - `decayed(cell: Cell | None, now: float) -> Cell`. None gives `Cell(PRIOR, PRIOR, now)`. Otherwise
      `alpha = PRIOR + (alpha - PRIOR) * 0.5 ** ((now - updated_at) / HALF_LIFE_S)`, the same for beta, and
      `updated_at = now`. Negative elapsed time counts as 0.
    - `updated(cell: Cell | None, engaged: bool, now: float) -> Cell`: decayed, then +1 to alpha or beta.
    - `mean(cell: Cell | None, now: float) -> float`: α/(α+β) after decay.
    - `evidence(cell: Cell | None, now: float) -> float`: α+β−2·PRIOR after decay.
    - `chance(cell: Cell | None, now: float) -> float`: `max(FLOOR, min(1.0, mean / 0.5))`.
  - **`CellStore` protocol:** `get_cell(kind, bucket) -> Cell | None`, `put_cell(kind, bucket, cell) -> None`.
    `MemoryCells` is the dict-backed implementation.
  - **`Twin(store: CellStore, clock)`:**
    - `record(kind, bucket, engaged: bool) -> None`. `ValueError` when the kind isn't in `KINDS`.
    - `p_engage(kind, bucket) -> float`.
    - `chance(kind, bucket) -> float`.
    - `should(kind, bucket, rng) -> bool`: `rng.random() < chance`.
    - `greet_style(bucket) -> Literal["short", "normal"]`: short when `p_engage("greeting", bucket) <
      SHORT_GREET_P`.
    - Reads never write.
  - **`tests/fakes.py`:** `FixedRng(value=0.0)`, whose `random()` returns `value`.

# Riya v0.11 "She uses what she knows" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** On a personal request ("pick a snack for me", "what do you know about my family"), her relevant facts reach the
prompt with no shared words, through a deterministic detector and a bounded lower-floor fill. The gate measures what
live Riya does, with the live bge embedder, pinned and verified.

**Architecture:**
- **Retrieval (`riya/core/memory.py`):**
  - `personal_request(query)` is a pure regex detector next to `about_me()`.
  - `NAMING` words stop counting as shared content.
  - `Memory.retrieve(..., personal=True)` adds up to 3 facts at cosine ≥ `personal_sim` on personal requests and
    records them in `Retrieved.filled`.
- **The embedder (`riya/core/embed.py`):** `FastEmbedder` carries `personal_sim = 0.45` and a pin (`EMBEDDER_PIN`):
  - `identity()` hashes the loaded snapshot;
  - `verify=True` raises on a mismatch;
  - live Riya only warns.
- **The engine:** a reply on the local route gets the fill and, when its block holds a fact, `personal_situation()`.
  Online turns, tools, greetings, bids, reports and games never do.
- **Offline measurement:**
  - `tests/data/personal_set.json` is the dev set, written blind.
  - `tests/data/bge_vectors.npz` holds recorded bge vectors, and `evaluation/recorded_vectors.py` serves them.
  - `retrieval_eval --personal` reports recall and dragging on the dev set and the probe bank.
  - `style_metrics.irrelevant_fact_used` is the reply-level check.
- **The gate:**
  - `persona_eval --embedder` uses the live embedder and records `embedder_id`.
  - `gate.identity()` gains `embedder`, and `GATE_VERSION` is 0.11.0.
  - Verdicts carry `embedder_id` and `retrieval_sha256`.
  - `champion` refuses a run without the pinned embedder.
  - `gate_calibrate` refuses a bank from another embedder or retrieval.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, numpy, fastembed 0.9.0 (onnxruntime, CPU), pytest. The live runs (controller steps) use
llama-server on a Kaggle T4.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-riya-v0.11-uses-what-she-knows-design.md`. Its §4.7 (the builder-check
decisions) is binding and wins over §4.1–§4.6 and §5 where they differ. The builder-check notes are in
`scratch_runs/v0.11-builder-check.md`.

## Spec ambiguities resolved here (binding for this plan; 27 in all)
1. **The dev set's layout** (§4.6, §7 leave it to the builder):
   `{"version": 1, "facts": [{id, text}], "requests": [{id, kind, q, relevant, memory}], "negatives": [{id, kind, q, memory}]}`.
   - A request's `kind` is `applied` or `topical`.
   - A negative's `kind` is one of `direct`, `shared_history`, `leading`, `near_miss`, `small_talk`, `we`, with at least
     3 of each. `identity` and `remember` are also allowed.
   - `memory` lists the fact ids the question's own small memory holds, and `relevant` ⊆ `memory`.
2. **"No fact values shared with the bank"** is tested three ways:
   - no 6-word normalised window (`holdout.overlaps`);
   - no bank proper name (a capitalised, non-sentence-initial word in a bank fact) as a word anywhere in the dev set;
   - no content-token bigram shared between a dev fact and a bank fact.
3. **Authorship:**
   - A separate subagent writes Task 1 from its brief alone. It doesn't see §4.1–§4.2, `riya/core/memory.py`, or any
     other task of this plan.
   - The dev set is committed before Task 4 starts and never edited after (§4.7.1).
4. **A4's denominator** is `persona_eval.PERSONAL_PROBES`, 17 fixed conversation ids: the 8 `applied`, the 8 `open`,
   and `g_about_12` ("what can't i eat"). A4 is a printed report, not an assertion (§4.7.1).
5. **The bank's other numbers:**
   - A2 (no fire on the honesty and other banks), A5 and A10 are hard assertions.
   - A6's bank total (≤ 2) is asserted too. If it fails, the implementer stops for a ruling.
   - The only change the bank may cause is tightening the detector after a fire on an unknown or AI probe, which is
     ledgered (§4.7.1).
6. **`SCORER_FILES` gains both `riya/core/memory.py` and `riya/core/embed.py`.** `personal_sim`, the floor, lives in
   `embed.py`, and §4.7.2's intent is that any detector or floor change invalidates records and verdicts. This is
   stricter than the spec's list.
7. **Verdicts carry `retrieval_sha256`** (stricter than the spec):
   - It is the LF-normalised sha256 over `riya/core/memory.py` and `riya/core/embed.py`, from
     `gate.retrieval_sha256()`.
   - `gate_calibrate` requires it to equal this code's. A bank is otherwise re-scored across scorer changes (v0.10
     §4.6.5), but a retrieval change alters what the prompts held, so it needs new live runs.
8. **The pin's string format:** `pin_id(snapshot, files, version)` returns
   `f"{digest[:16]}:bge-small-en-v1.5-onnx-Q@{snapshot[:8]}+fastembed-{version}"`.
   - `digest` is the sha256 of the canonical JSON `{"snapshot", "files": {name: [size, sha256]}, "fastembed"}`.
   - The digest comes first because every mismatch message shows `str(x)[:12]`.
9. **What `identity()` hashes:** the five files the pin names, in the loaded snapshot directory
   (`TextEmbedding.model._model_dir`, a private attribute of fastembed 0.9.0, pinned). A missing file hashes as
   `(-1, "missing")`. The snapshot name is the directory's name, and the version is
   `importlib.metadata.version("fastembed")`.
10. **`embedder_id`:**
    - It comes from `persona_eval.make_embedder`. A `FastEmbedder` reports its `embedder_id`, which equals
      `EMBEDDER_PIN` when verified. `HashEmbedder.identity()` is `"hash-2048"`.
    - `persona_eval.run` writes it into its score. `champion.run_variant` checks it against the pair it passed, and the
      verdict stores it top-level.
11. **The champion's embedder** (stricter than §4.7.5):
    - Every mode that runs probes builds the embedder once, up front, with `make_embedder("auto")`, after the
      calibration check and before any server. The modes are a candidate run, `--dry-run`, `--verdict-out` and
      `--base-vs-base`.
    - It refuses with exit 2, and nothing changes, unless the id equals `gate.identity()["embedder"]`.
12. **`persona_eval.main` builds one embedder** for the whole invocation, every `--dials` set included, and passes it to
    each `run`. `run(..., embedder=None)` builds `make_embedder("auto")` itself, after its reachability check.
13. **The recorded vectors** live in `evaluation/recorded_vectors.py`, so `retrieval_eval` can use them.
    - The npz is `texts` (a numpy unicode array), `vectors` (float32, n × 384) and `embedder_id` (a 0-d unicode
      array).
    - The corpus is every text in the dev set, the probe bank (facts and non-speak-first turn texts) and
      `tests/data/retrieval_set.json` (facts, episode summaries and questions).
14. **The opt-in bge tests:**
    - They skip unless `RIYA_TEST_BGE_CACHE` names a directory holding `models--Qdrant--bge-small-en-v1.5-onnx-Q`.
    - They copy it into `tmp_path` first, so the env path stays read-only even if fastembed writes metadata.
    - They load it with `local_files_only=True`, through the real loader that `tests/real_paths.py` recorded.
15. **The fill's edges:**
    - It ignores `min_sim`, the eval override, and draws only live facts (`status in ('active','conflict')`).
    - It adds `min(PERSONAL_EXTRA, k_facts − len(eligible facts kept))`, so nothing when the eligible facts already fill
      `k_facts`.
    - `filled` holds those ids in rank order.
16. **The engine's condition:** `personal = kind == "reply" and not (game or tool or small or seeing) and route ==
    "local"`.
    - The situation gains `personal_situation()` when `personal and retrieved.facts and personal_request(user_text)`.
    - `_bid` and every other `_retrieve` caller pass nothing, which means `personal=False`.
17. **`personal_situation()`** returns the module constant `PERSONAL_SITUATION`, read at call time. Its "≤ 40 tokens"
    is tested as ≤ 160 characters.
18. **The `relevant` annotation** sits on the turn of each `g_about_*` conversation tagged `distractor`, as indices into
    `conv["facts"]`. `run_conversation` copies the fact *texts* onto the row as `relevant`. The six annotations are
    fixed in Task 6.
19. **`irrelevant_share(rows)`** sits beside `score()`, not inside it:
    - It reports `(share, n)` over answered rows that carry `relevant`, with `remember_aboutme` excluded.
    - It prints `Irrelevant facts used (about-me rows with a distractor): {k} of {n} ({share:.0%})`, or `n/a` when n is
      0.
20. **The bank retrieval report:**
    - Each turn is checked against its conversation's seeded facts only, so remembered facts aren't modelled.
    - `speak_first` turns are skipped for retrieval but still checked by the detector.
21. **`nonpersonal_identical`** is the share of questions where `personal_request` is False whose fact ids are equal
    with `personal=True` and with `personal=False`.
22. **`dragged`** counts filled facts not marked relevant. On the dev set the mean and max run over all requests, and on
    the bank it is a total over the about-me turns.
23. **The SFT guard** (§4.7.6): every `scenarios(seed)` for seeds 0, 1 and 2 at the default `n`, plus the
    `scenarios.py:262` template rendered with every `FOODS` value. None may be a `personal_request`.
24. **A16's report lines** come from a CPU function, `gate_calibrate.bank_info(rows)`:
    - base about-me recall on `PERSONAL_PROBES`;
    - unknown honesty;
    - the `irrelevant_share`.

    They are recorded under `info` and printed as information, never as criteria. The v0.10.1 bank moves to
    `scratch_runs/riya_v1/gate/bank-0.10.1/` before the new bank lands.
25. **The Kaggle script** (`scratch_runs/kaggle_gate/run_gate.py`, git-ignored, so a controller step):
    - It picks its mode with `gate.calibration_problem(base_sha256=BASE_SHA)`, not by whether the record file exists.
    - It ships the bge cache as `bge_cache.bin`, a tar without `files_metadata.json`.
26. **The README note** (§4.7.4) goes in the root `README.md` under "Setting Riya up". The online-routing limit
    (§4.7.9) is documented in `Memory.retrieve`'s and `RiyaCore._speak`'s docstrings and in that note.
27. **A7** means `test_memory_recall.py`, `test_prompt.py` and the hash tests in `test_retrieval_set.py` pass unedited.
    Under `HashEmbedder` (strong = weak = 0.2), §4.3 can't change a result.

### Task 1: The dev set, written blind (`tests/data/personal_set.json`)
**Controller:** dispatch a fresh subagent with **only this task's text** and the spec's §2, §3 and §4.6. It gets
nothing from §1 (its examples carry bank fact values), §4.1–§4.2 or §4.7, no other task of this plan, and no
`riya/core/memory.py`. Commit it before Task 4 starts.

**Files:**
- Create: `tests/data/personal_set.json` and `tests/core/test_personal_set.py`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Produces the file** (layout in Resolution 1). Later tasks read it as JSON:
  - `facts[*].text` starts with `"The user"`;
  - `requests[*]` is `{id, kind: "applied"|"topical", q, relevant: [fact id], memory: [fact id]}`;
  - `negatives[*]` is `{id, kind, q, memory: [fact id]}`.
- **Consumes:** `riya.train.holdout.normalize` and `overlaps`, and `evaluation/persona_probes.json`, which only the test
  reads.

**Brief (what the set is for):** Riya keeps facts about the user. A *personal request* asks her to use what she knows
without naming the fact. A plain reading of the request couldn't find the fact by shared words.
- **Applied requests:**
  - "pick … for me"; "you know what I like";
  - "what should I eat / watch / order";
  - "find me something I'd enjoy" and the like.
- **Topical requests:** "what do you know about my family", "describe my habits", "what have you noticed about me".
- **Negatives** must stay strict:
  - a direct attribute question, even in other words ("what did I call my bike again?");
  - a shared-history question ("what did I tell you about my landlord?");
  - a leading question ("you know my dog's name, right?");
